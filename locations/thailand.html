AI Automation in Thailand — FAQ

How is AI adoption growing in Thailand?

Thailand's GenAI market is projected to grow from $180M in 2024 to $1.77B by 2030. The Thailand 4.0 initiative is driving digital transformation across manufacturing, logistics, and services. Thailand is ASEAN's 2nd largest robotics market, making it a natural fit for AI automation.

What industries in Thailand benefit from AI?

Manufacturing, logistics, tourism, and financial services see the strongest AI ROI in Thailand. AI document processing is particularly valuable for customs and trade documentation given Thailand's position as a major ASEAN trade hub.

Does 41 Labs serve businesses in Thailand?
