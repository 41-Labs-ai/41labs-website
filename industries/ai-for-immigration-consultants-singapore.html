What it does inside your sale

Step by step, in your flow, not a generic chatbot script

  1. It answers the first message in minutes. Day or night, on your own WhatsApp number. The lead is messaging other agencies at the same time, so the first reply matters. It answers in English or the mixed English your leads type. If many of your leads write in Chinese, tell us before you count on it.
  2. It asks the qualifying questions, one at a time. Nationality, pass type, years in Singapore, income band, and who is applying. It gives no odds and makes no assessment. Pass types your form turns away, such as Work Permit, get a polite no, so your consultants spend their time on cases they can take.
  3. It quotes your fee and answers the objection. If you want the fee stated up front, it quotes from your price list. When a lead says another company promised them heaven and earth, it answers in your words, from the scripts and SOPs you give us, and says plainly that ICA decides.
  4. It books the consultation. Zoom or your office, whichever the lead prefers. The lead picks a time, the calendar invite goes out, and the lead can change the time in the same chat. Once the consultation is booked, the agent stops selling and your consultant takes over.
  5. It follows up until the lead replies. It tries again a few hours later and keeps trying over the following week, until they answer or ask it to stop. "Get back to me next week" sets a reminder for next week. Leads nobody has chased in months can be messaged again from an export of your old chats, with a WhatsApp approved message.

What it connects to. On Starter it reads your price list, your scripts and SOPs, and your calendar. On Pro it also writes each lead and booked consultation into your CRM, if it is one we have connected before, and can pass the record on by webhook to a tool like Zapier. It does not read your Facebook lead form and it does not connect to Privyr today, so ask us before you count on either. Email is not a channel. WhatsApp group chats are not either, that is a WhatsApp limit.
