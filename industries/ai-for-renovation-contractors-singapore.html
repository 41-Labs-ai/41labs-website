What it does inside your sale

Step by step, in your flow, not a generic chatbot script

  1. It answers the first question from your own answers. Over existing tiles, outdoors, waterproof, how long it takes, what the warranty covers. You give us the answers once, the way you already say them, and it gives them back in seconds at any hour. It does not make up an answer you did not give it.
  2. It asks what you would ask before pricing. Floor, walls or both. Rough area. What is there now. HDB or condo. Which finish. Then it asks for a photo or the floor plan, because that is what you need before you can price.
  3. It holds your price. If you price per point or per square foot, it gives your rate and works out the customer's own total from it. If you only quote after a site visit, it says so, explains what moves the price, and books the visit instead. Either way it never invents a number.
  4. It books the site visit. It offers the slots from your calendar, confirms, and sends your team the address, the photo and what the customer wants. A shop or cafe job gets flagged to you as commercial.
  5. It chases the quote that went quiet. Nine days after a quote goes out with no reply, it checks in and offers start weeks when the customer comes back. One honest limit: after 24 hours of silence WhatsApp only allows an approved template, and Meta charges for those conversations. We set the template up, and Meta's fee is billed separately.

What it connects to. On Starter it reads what you give us: your price list or per-point rate card, your catalogue, your usual answers and your calendar link. On Pro it connects to your own systems. For this trade that has meant the Google Sheet or Excel tracker where quotes and maintenance jobs are logged, read and written back, and the Google Calendar your team books site visits into. Pro also takes the deposit in the chat.
