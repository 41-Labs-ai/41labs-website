What it does inside your sale

Step by step, in your flow, not a generic chatbot script

  1. It finds the piece from a photo or a few words. The buyer sends a photo or types flower pendant, 18K white gold. The agent searches the whole catalogue on what the record holds and on what is visible in the photo, and replies in one message with the closest pieces, their photos and the prices in your record. When there are too many matches it asks one narrowing question, budget or diamond size, before it shows more.
  2. It says which outlet holds it. Stock sits per outlet in the catalogue, so the reply says the piece is at your Tampines shop today, or that it is one of a kind. In the demo we built, a piece that has sold is hidden from search, and a second buyer asking for it is told it has just been taken.
  3. It holds your price and takes the deposit. The agent quotes only what the record says. Pushed for a better price, it holds. When the buyer wants a person, it hands over, and the price stays where you set it. To hold a piece it sends a payment link for a deposit. Once paid, the piece is marked reserved and the outlet gets a WhatsApp message to take it off the counter.
  4. It hands over what it should not answer. Pawn ticket questions and valuations of a customer's own gold go to your staff, with the chat so far. Gold by weight is handled the same way on Starter: the agent says the team will share the live rate and pings your staff. On Pro a live rate source can be connected, ask us before you count on it.
  5. It follows up and keeps the lead. Every buyer's name and number are stored from the first message. A buyer who paid a deposit but has not come down is reminded. A buyer who stopped replying is followed up. Your staff see the whole chat when they take over, so the buyer never repeats the story.

What it connects to. On Starter it reads a catalogue file you export: item ID, title, description, purity, weight, carat, price, photo and the outlet that holds the piece, plus a daily list of what sold. On Pro it connects to your online store or stock system, so a counter sale takes the piece out of the chat and a WhatsApp reservation is written back. If your stock lives in custom in-house software and your IT will not open it, even read-only, start on Starter with the daily file. That is what the group we spoke to chose.

Can an AI agent search a jewellery catalogue of 100,000 pieces on WhatsApp?

Yes, if the catalogue is in a file or a store it can read. The agent does not scan every record on each message. The catalogue is indexed once, then searched by category and by what is visible in the photo. The group we spoke to sent 100 pieces as they were, bare titles and small thumbnails, and we built the demo on those without a re-shoot. Hard facts like purity, weight and carat come from your record only, the agent never guesses them.

What stops a double sale, where the shop sells a piece someone has just bought online?

On Starter the agent takes a deposit by payment link and marks the piece reserved so no other buyer is offered it. Your outlet gets a WhatsApp message to take it off the counter. Between your daily sold file and that message there is still a window of up to a day. On Pro the agent connects to your stock system, so a counter sale takes the piece out of the chat within the conversation. Does it quote the live gold price?

For diamond and gem pieces it quotes the price in your record. For gold by weight on Starter it tells the buyer the team will share the live rate and hands the chat to your staff. Can it tell a customer which outlet has the piece?

Yes, when your catalogue file or stock system holds the outlet for each piece. The reply names the outlet and says if the piece is one of a kind. If your stock file does not record the outlet, the agent says the team will confirm and pings your staff.

Can it answer pawn ticket questions?

No. The agent does not value a customer's gold and does not answer questions about a pawn ticket or its due date.
