Your counter closes at 6, and at 9pm a contractor messages for the price and stock of a part he needs on site by 8am. 41 Closer answers from your own catalogue, checks stock, takes the deposit and passes the order to your team before you open.

Message the agent on WhatsApp

By Alexander Lee, founder of 41 Labs. Last updated 10 October 2026. Written from our calls with eight suppliers and distributors, from a multi-branch hardware wholesaler and a steel supplier to an FMCG distributor, an electronics distributor and a lighting and bathroom showroom.

41 Closer is a managed AI sales agent on WhatsApp for suppliers and distributors in Singapore. It finds the product a contractor asks for by name, code or photo, quotes from your catalogue, checks stock per branch, applies your delivery and deposit rules, takes payment in the chat and passes the confirmed order to your team, 24 hours a day.

A hardware or materials supplier gets the same messages every night: got stock, how much, which branch, can deliver tomorrow, can quote 10 pieces. One multi-branch hardware supplier with about 40,000 products told us it gets 30 to 50 WhatsApp enquiries a day, and before the agent every message after 5pm was lost. Our own test found the same pattern across Singapore: a human reply at night takes a median of 13.3 hours. 41 Closer answers in seconds. What it does inside your sale

Step by step, in your flow, not a generic chatbot script

  1. It finds the product. By name, product code or a photo, from your own catalogue. It shows the price, the picture and the product link. If the term is ambiguous (safety boots or safety shoes), it asks one question. If an item has no price on your list, it says the team will confirm and hands over. It never invents a price.
  2. It checks stock and adds up the list. On Pro it reads stock per branch from AutoCount, so a contractor hears which branch has the item tonight. Send it a list of eight anchors with quantities and it searches each one, prices each one and shows a running total with the items it could not find flagged.
  3. It applies your delivery and deposit rules. Free delivery above a threshold you set, a fee below it, the areas your driver does not go, void deck only for HDB blocks. Below S$200 it just collects payment, above S$1,000 it collects a deposit first, if that is your rule. On Pro the payment link goes out in the chat.
  4. It confirms the order and passes it to your team. Your team gets the order summary on WhatsApp within the minute: customer, company, items, quantities, prices, delivery address, deposit status. On Pro the order is written back to your system so nobody keys it in twice. An account customer, a credit request, a discount request or a same-day job goes straight to a salesperson with the order already written up.
  5. It follows up and hands over. A quote nobody answered gets a follow-up after 24 hours and again after a week, naming the items. A complaint, a return, a project price or anything outside your catalogue goes to a person. When a customer asks for a person, it connects them and stops.

What it connects to. On Starter it reads the price list or product export you give us, your collection addresses, your delivery rules and your deposit rules, and it sends every order summary to your team on WhatsApp. On Pro it reads your Shopify catalogue and the stock and price per branch in AutoCount, takes the deposit through HitPay or the payment link you already use, and writes the confirmed order and a CRM note back. SAP, Odoo and other ERPs we scope first and price in writing before you decide.

What suppliers and distributors said to us, and our answer

Paraphrased from our calls. No business is named.

"I worry they take my data and sell it to my competitors."

Your catalogue, your prices and your chat history sit in your own workspace, and we sign an NDA before we see a file. Nobody else sees your price list and no other client's agent can read it. If a vendor cannot put that in writing, do not give them your price list.

"My staff search faster by hand than any AI. They know every code."

On day one that is often true for a counter veteran who knows every part. The agent works the hours your staff are off the counter, 6pm to 8am and the weekend, when nobody is searching at all. The hardware supplier started it after hours only and trained the staff before widening it, and you can do the same.

"Don't be helpful. I want it to sell."

We agree. Once it has found the item it asks for the quantity, then the delivery address, then it applies your deposit rule and sends the payment link. On anything it cannot settle on its own, an account price or credit terms, it hands over with the order already written up so your salesperson closes rather than starts again.

"Our IT will not open a port to the accounting server. Too dangerous."

You do not have to. We read stock through your accounting vendor's web API or a scheduled export, and nothing opens into your server. If your vendor has no API yet, Starter runs on the price and stock export you send us until they do.

"Our dealers order by email and phone. They do not use WhatsApp."

Then do not buy this. 41 Closer is a WhatsApp agent, and if your trade buyers do not message you there, it will sit idle. A shared inbox with a proper order form will serve you better until your buyers move. We told a power track distributor the same thing when their leads came in by email and phone.

"We do not want unforeseen expenses."

The price is the plan, and a one-time S$2,400 if you connect a system we have connected before. There is no setup fee. One month to start, then month to month with 30 days notice. A system we have not connected before gets scoped and priced in writing before you say yes.

AI agent for suppliers and distributors: questions owners ask

Can an AI agent read my stock in AutoCount and quote on WhatsApp?

Yes, on Pro. It reads stock and price per branch from AutoCount through the vendor's web API or a scheduled export. It quotes the price on your list and never invents one. If an item has no price, it tells the customer the team will confirm and hands over. Connecting AutoCount is a one-time S$2,400 because we have connected it before.

What happens when a contractor sends a photo of a part at 9pm?

It identifies the product from the photo and searches your catalogue. It shows the closest matches with price and picture. If it is not sure, it says so and shows the options. If the part is not in your catalogue, it passes the photo to your team.

Will it give my wholesale price to a walk-in customer?

No. It quotes the retail price you give it. When a customer says they have an account, it stops quoting and passes the full order to your salesperson to apply the account price. If you want it to quote account prices itself, we scope that with you first.

How does it handle delivery charges and deposits?

You give it the rules. For example, free delivery above S$300, a fee below that, the areas your driver does not go, and void deck only for HDB blocks. Below S$200 it just collects payment and above S$1,000 it collects a deposit first, if that is your rule. On Pro the payment link goes out in the chat. A same-day request goes to your team.

Does it key the order into my system or does my admin still do it?

On Starter it sends the order summary to your team on WhatsApp and your admin keys it in. On Pro it writes the confirmed order back to your system and notifies your team. An FMCG distributor told us it has two or three admin staff per company just keying WhatsApp orders into SAP. That is the step Pro removes.
