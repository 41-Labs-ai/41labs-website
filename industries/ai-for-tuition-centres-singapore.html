What it does inside your sale

Step by step, in your flow, not a generic chatbot script

  1. It answers the intake and schedule question. Which level, which day, which branch, and when the next intake starts, from the timetable you give us. One chain we spoke to wanted a nearest-branch finder for parents, so the agent asks where they stay and names the branch. For an institute it gives the intake dates and the entry requirement for the programme asked about.
  2. It gives the right fee. A tuition centre's term fee comes straight from the fee sheet, with the term payment option if you offer one. An institute's funded fee depends on age, citizenship, employment and sponsorship, so the agent asks those first and quotes from your funding table, the way we built it for one training institute. It never guesses a fee it has not been given.
  3. It asks one qualifying question at a time. Local or international, which programme, which child, which branch. When a student writes that they got a D7 for English, it knows the alternative entry route your rules allow instead of sending a menu. If three messages arrive in a row it reads all of them and replies once.
  4. It books the trial lesson or the consultation. On Starter it sends your booking link. On Pro it writes the trial lesson or the career counselling appointment into your calendar and sends the address and what to bring. For institutes it books the consultant and stops there, because enrolment and the funding tier are confirmed by a person on documents.
  5. It follows up and hands over. A parent who goes quiet gets a follow-up. A question it has not been taught is paused and sent to your admin, and the answer is remembered for next time. Your team gets the chat with the context already gathered.

What it connects to. On Starter it reads what you send us: the fee sheet, the timetable by level and branch, the intake dates, the funding table and your booking link. On Pro it reads and writes your own systems. For the institutes we built for, that was the recruitment team's calendar, so the appointment lands in it, and the CRM the team already uses, so the lead and the chat arrive with the context. For a tuition chain it can take the term fee by payment link in the chat, and if you want it to answer top-up questions from the real credit balance, that is a connection to the system that holds it, and we tell you the price before we build it. Course fees that are invoiced stay invoiced, and the agent books the consultant instead.

AI agent for tuition centres: questions owners ask

What does an AI agent do for a tuition centre in Singapore?

It answers parents on your WhatsApp number at any hour. It tells them which level and day is available, which branch is nearest, what the term fee is and whether it can be paid by term, from the timetable and fee sheet you give it. It books the trial lesson and hands the chat to your admin with the answers already given. 41 Closer is built and run by 41 Labs, so there is no tool for you to set up.

Can it quote the fee, or does it only take a message?

It quotes the fee from your fee sheet, the same way your admin would. If the fee depends on the level, the branch or the number of lessons, it asks which one first. If it has not been given a fee it says so and asks your admin rather than guessing.

Can it book a trial lesson or an appointment into our calendar?

Yes. On Starter it sends your booking link and follows up if the parent does not book. On Pro it writes the trial lesson or the counselling appointment into your calendar and sends the address and what to bring. For the private education institutes we built for, the appointment goes into the recruitment team's calendar and the consultant takes it from there.

We are a private education institute with funded fees. Can it quote the subsidised fee?

It quotes from your funding table once it has asked the questions the fee depends on, such as age, citizenship, employment status and whether an employer is sponsoring. That is how we built it for one training institute, where the fee for the same course changes with age and citizenship. Confirming the funding tier on documents stays with your consultant, so the agent books that appointment instead of enrolling anyone. Course fees that are invoiced stay invoiced.

How much does 41 Closer cost for a tuition centre?

Starter is S$690 a month and Pro is S$1,490 a month, with the build included and no setup fee. Connecting a system of your own that we have connected before is a one-time S$2,400. You pay one month to start, charged from go-live, then month to month with 30 days notice and no contract. No GST is charged, and Meta's per-conversation WhatsApp fees are billed separately. Eligible Singapore SMEs can apply the EDGE grant, which replaced the EDG on 30 September 2026, to part of the cost, up to 70% of qualifying costs.

Does it reply in Chinese or Malay?

It replies in English and in the mixed English that Singapore parents and students type. If a share of your enquiries come in Chinese or Malay, tell us before we build and we test it with you first rather than promising it. One institute we met offers courses in Chinese and Malay, and that is exactly the case we would test on your own messages before go-live.

What happens when a parent asks something it has not been taught?

It pauses, tells the parent it will check, and sends the question to your admin on WhatsApp. When your admin answers, the agent replies to the parent and remembers the answer for the next time. Fee exemptions, appeals and anything that needs a judgement call go to a person every time.

How fast can it be live, and what do we need to send?

Your timetable by level and branch, your fee sheet, your intake dates and the policies you want it to hold, such as term payment and make-up lessons. We build the agent from that before we ever get on a call, and it is live within 48 hours of the list. You message it yourself first, then we spend one free hour walking you through the platform and connecting your payments and your WhatsApp number. Billing starts the day it goes live.
