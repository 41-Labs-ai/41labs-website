What it does inside your sale

Step by step, in your flow, not a generic chatbot script

  1. It answers the cost question from your own fee sheet. Levy, salary range by nationality, agency fee, insurance and the security bond, all from the figures you give it, labelled as a guide. The one agency we built for publishes its fee table, so the agent quotes that table and nothing else. It never gives a MOM approval date, because that is not yours to promise either.
  2. It asks one qualifying question and matches from approved biodata. Does mum need help getting around, or mostly cooking and company? Infant care or school-age children? Then it sends the profiles your matching team has approved for that need, with the recorded video interview. It does not describe a helper beyond her biodata. It says 'she is good with babies' only if that line is on the biodata you approved.
  3. It books the interview with a relationship manager on the call. It offers times from your interview calendar and confirms the slot. On Starter that is your calendar link. On Pro it writes the interview into your booking system and the family's details into your CRM. The family gets a reminder before the call.
  4. It offers the transfer route when the family needs someone soon. A helper already in Singapore means no flight. The agent says that, puts the profile on hold for the relationship manager, and leaves the timing to your team. One agency we built for publishes a 2 to 3 week range for transfers. The agent can repeat a range you publish, as your guide, and never turns it into a promise.
  5. It hands over when the case needs a person. A father with dementia, or a family that had a bad experience with a previous helper. The agent says so plainly, takes the family's name and number, and tells your relationship manager the same minute. It does not give a quick yes on a case like that.

What it connects to. On Starter it reads your fee sheet (levy, salary bands by nationality, agency fee, insurance, bond, replacement terms), the nationalities you actually place, the biodata you send it as a list with video interview links, and your interview calendar link. On Pro it reads your live biodata catalogue so it only offers helpers who are still available, writes the interview into your booking system, puts a profile on hold, logs the family in your CRM, and takes the deposit or agency fee in the chat. It does not file MOM paperwork. Your admin team does that, and the agent tells the family which documents to have ready.

No. It answers fees and process only, and it never gives an approval date.
