What it does inside your sale

Step by step, in your flow, not a generic chatbot script

  1. It reads the request and asks only for what is missing. Route, dates, passenger count and cabin. For a tour: dates, group size, hotel tier and nationality, because the visa rule differs by passport. One question at a time, in the customer's own words, and nothing asked twice.
  2. It prices on your own systems. On your Amadeus office it returns up to three options with times, bag allowance and total, or the whole day's schedule when the boss wants to pick the timing. For a tour it quotes your 3-star package by group size, with the inclusions, and tells the customer a person will confirm rooms on a 4 or 5-star upgrade.
  3. It answers the fare question from the airline's rule. Refundable or not, the change fee and how long the ticket is valid, quoted word for word from the published fare rule. It never rounds and never converts the currency. Processing a refund or a reissue stays with a consultant.
  4. It takes the details and holds the seats. Traveller name, passport and the approval code if your client needs one. It holds the seats and returns the booking reference. For a tour it takes the 50% deposit and notes the balance due 40 days before departure, your own terms.
  5. It hands over and follows up. A consultant issues the ticket and takes payment, by BSP or card, the way you do now. Lounge and visa questions, group trips, budget carriers and anything it is unsure of go to a person. A quiet prospect gets a follow-up on a schedule that stops the moment they reply.

What it connects to. On Starter it reads the package list, rate sheet and fare notes you give us and sends your own booking or deposit link. On Pro it reads your Amadeus office for live fares, schedules and fare rules, holds seats and looks up a booking by reference and surname. For a tour operator, Pro reads your reservation system for packages, festival dates and prices by group size, writes the booking back and takes the deposit in the chat. Your shared inbox tool, accounting system and email are separate steps we scope with you.

What travel agencies and tour operators said to us, and our answer

Paraphrased from our calls. No business is named.

"Our clients mostly email us. WhatsApp is a minority."

Then be careful. 41 Closer works on WhatsApp, and if your clients will only ever email, do not buy it for that desk. One agency with mostly email clients chose to start on its WhatsApp line anyway and look at email later, because the urgent and after-hours requests already arrive there. Email as a second channel is a separate piece of work we scope with you.

"Lounge access, visas, membership rules. They change every week. It will guess and cost me a client."

It does not guess. Lounge, membership and visa questions go to a person every time, by rule, and the desk we built for one agency refuses them on purpose. Your ops head's rules go in as rules, so the three-hour connection limit or the six-month passport check holds at 11pm without anyone remembering it.

"I am not letting a bot issue tickets or charge a card."

Neither are we. The desk prices the flight, states the fare rule and holds the seats. A consultant releases the booking, issues the ticket and takes the payment, by BSP or card, as they do today. That was the agency's own decision on our last call and it is how the demo runs.

"Someone books with a stolen card, the ticket is issued, and the chargeback lands on us a week later."

This does not fix card fraud, and we will not pretend it does. The agent never asks for a card number in the chat, and ticketing stays with your consultant and your checks. If a chargeback every month or two is your biggest cost, a payment provider's fraud screening is the first thing to look at, before an AI agent.

"We have contracted fares with the airlines. Your prices will look wrong next to my terminal."

They will, until the contracted fares are switched on. Out of the box it searches published fares on your own office ID. Tell us which airlines you hold private fares with before your team tests it, and we start there.

"Every trip we sell is custom. People arrive with a ChatGPT itinerary that does not work."

That is the case it was built for at one tour operator. It asks who is travelling and how many days they have, then recommends one of your own packages for that length of trip, at your price for that group size. A 4 or 5-star upgrade goes to a person to confirm rooms, and it will not sell a festival trip inside three months of the date, because your rule says the rooms are gone.

AI agent for travel agencies and tour operators: questions owners ask

Can an AI agent search live flights on my Amadeus for a WhatsApp enquiry?

Yes. The desk we built for one Singapore agency searches and prices on the agency's own office ID, any airline in the GDS. It returns up to three options with times, bag allowance and total, or the whole day's schedule with open booking classes. It also prices child and infant fares. Amadeus gives test access first and certifies the connection before production, so plan for that step.

Can it tell my client the cancellation fee and the change fee?

Yes, from the airline's published fare rule. It states whether the fare is refundable, the reissue fee and how long the ticket is valid, in the airline's own words, never rounded and never converted. Stating the condition is the agent's job. Processing the refund or the change stays with a consultant.

Will it issue tickets or take card payments?

No. It holds the seats and returns the booking reference, then a consultant issues the ticket and takes payment by BSP or card. That is how the agency we built it for wanted it, and it is how the demo runs. Ticketing through the connection can be switched on later, once Amadeus certifies it and only if you ask for it.
