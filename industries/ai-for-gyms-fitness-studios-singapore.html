What it does inside your sale

Step by step, in your flow, not a generic chatbot script

  1. It answers the price question with a conversation. A customer asks how much for personal training. The agent answers with a question before a number. It says a coach comes to their home or condo, then asks what they are hoping to work on. This is the flow we built for the in-home trainer whose price enquiries went cold.
  2. It qualifies the lead. Who the training is for and what the goal is. Then the area, so you know which coach is near. For a kids enquiry it asks the child's age and the sport. You tell us the questions a coach needs answered before a first session, and it asks them.
  3. It quotes from your price list, or books the consultation if you have none. If you give us your session rate and your packs, it quotes them once it knows the goal. If you price every programme after a consultation, it says so and will not invent a figure even when the customer pushes for a ballpark. We tested that push before the trainer's demo and it still gave no figure.
  4. It books the consultation or trial slot. It takes the day, the time window and the best number to reach them. It confirms the booking back in one message. A 'New consultation booked' card then lands in your inbox for a coach to action. On Pro the slot is read from and written into your own booking system.
  5. It follows up and hands over. A customer who goes quiet after the price gets a follow-up. A question it has no answer for, such as your cancellation policy or a coach's qualifications, goes to a person, and the customer is told a coach will reply. Your team sees every chat in one inbox.

What it connects to. On Starter it reads your price list, your class or session list, your packs, the areas you cover and your calendar link. It writes nothing into any system. On Pro it connects to your own booking or membership system. It reads the live slots and writes the booking back. It can take the pack payment in the chat. The trainer we built the demo for ran no system at all, so the demo booked into the inbox only.
