You lead a property team, your agents get listing enquiries at 11pm asking still available and can view this weekend, and the follow-up stops after one message. 41 Closer answers in seconds, asks the questions you want answered before a call, then books the viewing and chases the buyer who goes quiet.

What your WhatsApp looks like at 9pm

The messages that land on your agents' phones, the way buyers type them

  • "still available?"
  • "what floor?"
  • "can view this weekend?"
  • "what is the psf?"
  • "got agent fee?"
  • "how to register for the new launch?"

Every one of these is a fact from the listing or a request for a slot. None of them needs an agent's judgement, and all of them arrive while your agents are at a viewing, on a call or at a showflat. The new-launch agent we spoke to pays for every one of these messages, about S$15 a click on Google and S$3,000 to S$4,000 a month on Meta ads, so an enquiry that waits until morning has already been paid for.

What it does inside your sale

Step by step, in your flow, not a generic chatbot script

  1. It answers the listing question. Still available, what floor, size, asking price, psf, agent fee, launch date. It answers from the listing sheet you give it and nothing else. If the answer is not on the sheet it says it will check and hands the chat to the agent.
  2. It asks your qualifying questions. Budget, bedrooms, own stay or investment, whether they own a property now, which area. The team leader we spoke to wanted exactly these filled in before any call, so the agents stop asking them by voice note. One question at a time, in the buyer's own words back to them.
  3. It books the viewing or the call. On Starter it sends your calendar link and confirms the slot the buyer picks. On Pro it books straight into the listing agent's calendar and writes the buyer into your CRM, so the Zoom scheduling the team leader described happens without an agent touching it.
  4. It chases the buyer who goes quiet. A buyer who asked for a weekend slot and stopped replying gets a short follow-up, then another, until the viewing is confirmed or they say no. This is the step the team leader told us slips when the week gets busy.
  5. It hands over anything that needs a licence. Owner can nego, what is my flat worth, what should I offer: all of it goes to your registered salesperson with the full chat. The agent does not negotiate, does not value and does not advise on an offer.

What it connects to. On Starter it reads your listing sheet (availability, floor, size, asking price, psf, your agent fee rule, launch dates and showflat hours) and your calendar link. On Pro it writes each qualified buyer into your CRM and books viewings and calls straight into the listing agent's calendar. Connecting a system we have connected before is a one-time S$2,400. It does not read property portals, so your listing sheet is what it works from.

What property agents said to us, and our answer

Paraphrased from our calls. No business is named.

"My associates already auto-blast messages tailored to each client. Is this not the same thing?"

No. A blast is one message to many people, and when a buyer replies nobody is there to answer. 41 Closer replies to one buyer about one listing and asks the next question until a viewing is booked or the buyer says no. If all you want is to send a broadcast, a WhatsApp broadcast list or a blast tool is cheaper and you should use that instead.

"I follow up with voice notes. It is more personal than a typed reply."

Keep doing that. The agent does not send voice notes and does not make calls, it types. It gets the buyer to the point where your voice note is worth sending, with the qualifying questions already answered. You send the voice note to a buyer with a viewing booked, not to one who asked what floor and vanished.

"My associate tried an AI tool and the summary it gave was not exact."

That happens when a tool is left to guess. 41 Closer answers only from the listing sheet you give it, and when a question is outside the sheet it says it will check and hands the chat to the agent. You see every handover with the full conversation. It can still get something wrong, which is one more reason price and offers are kept away from it.

"I already pay S$9,000 a month for a marketing team plus ads. I cannot add a cost before the commission comes in."

Then look at what happens to the enquiries you already pay for. That agent had 35 enquiries land in the month before one launch, and 41 Closer does not make more enquiries, it answers the ones you have. If your problem is too few leads, spend on ads, this is not it. If the problem is leads landing and going cold, this is it, at S$690 a month with no contract.

"CEA will not let a bot negotiate for me."

Correct, and it does not. The agent answers listing facts, asks your qualifying questions, books the viewing and chases. The moment a buyer asks whether the owner can negotiate, or a seller asks what their flat is worth, it says that is for the agent and hands over the chat. Your registered salesperson does every part of the job that needs a licence.

"I have a few listings and I reply fast myself."

Then do not buy this. One agent with a handful of listings who replies within minutes loses nothing to a slow reply. This is for a team leader whose agents each carry listings and ad spend and cannot be on their phones at every viewing.

Our agents get lots of enquiries about property listings. What should we do with them?

Answer each one within minutes, ask the qualifying questions before anyone calls, and book the viewing in the same chat. That is what 41 Closer does on your team's WhatsApp number. Your agents then spend their time on buyers who have a budget, a slot and a reason to move. The buyer who asked what floor at 11pm gets an answer at 11pm instead of the next afternoon.

Yes, as long as it stays on listing facts and bookings. 41 Closer answers availability, floor, size, asking price, psf and agent fee from the sheet you give it. It does not negotiate, does not give an opinion on value and does not advise on an offer.
