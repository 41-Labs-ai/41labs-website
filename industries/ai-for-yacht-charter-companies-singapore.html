What it does inside your sale

Step by step, in your flow, not a generic chatbot script

  1. It answers the availability question. It knows your slots, for example a morning and an evening slot every day, and it answers the date question first because that is what the customer asked. On Starter it works from the calendar you give us and your team confirm the slot. On Pro it reads your booking engine and only offers a slot that is open. In the agent we built, it is blocked from saying a date is free or taken when it cannot see a calendar.
  2. It puts the group on the right boat and quotes the real price. The occasion, the guest count and the date decide the boat. It recommends one boat with a reason and gives the price for the slot in the same message. It sends the photo too, because people choose a boat with their eyes. Then it offers the one or two add-ons that fit, BBQ for a family day, DJ for a party, each with its own price. If two of your boats are the same model at the same price, it says so and does not invent a difference. If your boats leave from different marinas, it names the right one for the boat chosen.
  3. It answers the policy questions on the spot. What if it rains, can we fish, can we bring our own food, can we bring the dog, how many can come, what time do we arrive. These are your published rules, so it answers them in the first reply and then asks for the date. It never hands a published policy to a human.
  4. It takes the deposit and writes the booking. It recaps the boat, the date, the slot, the guest count and each add-on with its price, then sends the deposit or full payment link, whichever is your rule. On Pro the booking is written into your booking engine and your CRM, and your team are notified in the same turn with everything they need for the day.
  5. It hands over what it should not price, and follows up the rest. A group above your guest cap, or an event you price by hand, gets the date, the headcount and a contact captured, then goes to your team with a note that a real number is coming. It does not guess. Anyone who goes quiet gets a follow-up, and a no on this date is left open for the next one.

What it connects to. On Starter it reads your boat list with photos, your prices per slot, your add-on list, your policies and the calendar link you give us. Bookings and deposits go to your team. On Pro it reads availability from your booking engine, writes the booking back, posts the customer and the booking to your CRM, and sends the deposit or payment link in the chat. The operator we built for runs a Rezdy booking engine and asked for Zoho CRM. Connecting a system we have connected before is a one-time S$2,400. A booking engine we have not connected before is scoped on the call first.

"My admin already answers the same twenty questions all day. She is at capacity. Even 10% more enquiries would break us."

That is the case this was built for. The agent takes the repeat questions, which boat, which slot, how many guests, what is included, what if it rains, and only passes your admin the booking or the enquiry it cannot price. What it does not fix is the work after the booking, such as crew rostering and provisioning. If that is where your admin is stuck, this will not help, and we will say so on the call.

"Will my admin think this is here to replace her?"

She might, and it is worth saying out loud with her before go-live. The agent answers the first twenty messages. She still confirms the slots and handles the changes, and the corporate clients still talk to her. The operator who raised this had an admin who was the bottleneck, so the aim was to give her the evenings back.

"Our invoicing and payment is still manual. Can it do that?"

On Pro it sends the deposit or payment link in the chat and writes the booking into your booking engine or CRM, so nobody types the invoice. On Starter it hands the agreed booking to your team and they take payment the way they do today. If invoicing is the only thing you want fixed and you are happy with how enquiries are answered now, a booking engine with payment built in is cheaper than this. We will tell you that on the call.

"We do not have a live calendar. Where would it get availability from?"

Then it does not pretend to. In the agent we built for an operator with no published calendar, it takes the date, quotes the charter and tells the customer the team confirm the slot once the booking is in. It is built to refuse to say a date is free or taken when it cannot see one. If you keep availability in a booking engine, Pro reads it live and the guessing stops.

"We want everything in our CRM."

On Pro it writes the booking and the customer into your CRM. The operator we spoke to runs Zoho, and that is the kind of system we connect. On Starter you get a summary handed to your team instead, with the date, the guest count, the boat and the contact.

"Can it collect reviews with a QR code on board?"

No. It answers and books over WhatsApp. A Google review link on a printed QR code does the on-board review job, and it costs you nothing. Do not buy this for reviews.
