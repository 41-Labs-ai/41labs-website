What it does inside your sale

Step by step, in your flow, not a generic chatbot script

  1. It answers the price question first. A named treatment gets the published from-price, before GST, and the consultation fee in the same reply. It says the number of sessions is the doctor's call. It never offers a discount, a gift or a package deal in chat.
  2. It asks two or three questions, then points to one treatment on your menu. Where, how long, what has been tried. Then one treatment from your published list, with the reason in plain words, and a line that the doctor confirms suitability at the consultation. It also asks whether there is anything else the doctor should look at in the same visit. It will not assess a photo. It says so and offers the consultation.
  3. It offers a slot and secures it. On Starter it sends your booking link. On Pro it reads your calendar, offers real times, holds the slot and collects the consultation fee in the chat. The clinic demo asked for S$55 to secure the slot, which was that clinic's published fee.
  4. It chases the enquiry that goes quiet. If the patient stops replying six to ten minutes after a price, the agent sends one nudge on its own. For enquiries that went cold months ago, it runs a campaign on Meta-approved templates with an opt-out on every message, from the contact export you give us.
  5. It hands over the moment it should. A lump, swelling or pain after a treatment is urgent. The agent gives the clinic phone and alerts your team in the same turn, and your phone buzzes. You can pause the agent on any chat and take over yourself in the inbox.

What it connects to. On Starter it reads your treatment list with published prices, your opening hours and your booking link, and hands over to your desk in the inbox. On Pro it reads your Google Calendar for open consultation slots, writes the booking back and takes the consultation fee in the chat. If you run a salon POS for bookings and commissions, we quote the connection, and a system we have connected before is a one-time S$2,400. What aesthetic clinics said to us, and our answer

Paraphrased from our calls. No business is named.

"Can it diagnose or recommend a treatment?"

No, by design. It quotes the published price, points to the treatment on your menu for the concern the patient named, and says the doctor decides at the consultation. It will not assess a photo, promise a result or predict how many sessions. That is the line we built to, and it is the reason a doctor-led clinic can run it at all.

"We are on respond.io today. Can we keep it?"

One WhatsApp number can only sit on one API connection. So the patient-facing number moves to the agent, respond.io keeps your other channels, or a second number runs the agent. Your contacts come over as an export that we load for the first reactivation campaign. There is no live sync between the two systems.

"I want to overbook trials by two or three because people do not show up."

The agent books into the slots your calendar has open. Overbooking is not a feature. If you want it, we add two or three standby calendars per centre so bookings can go over capacity, and your desk manages the overlap on the day. We say that before you sign, not after.

"Can it reschedule, send reminders and show me every follow-up it sent?"

It books. A cancellation or a change goes to your desk, not the agent. Appointment reminders are not built in today, and the report shows campaign sent and replied counts plus a stats bar, not a per-message log of every follow-up. If those three are the reason you are buying, wait.

"Our weekday chairs are under 40% full and all our marketing is Instagram."

Then an agent is the wrong first purchase. It only answers people who already message you. The salon that told us this got a website and search work from us instead, because its first problem was being found. Come back when WhatsApp fills up faster than your desk can clear it.

"Patients ask for discounts and instalment plans all the time."

It quotes the published price and never offers a discount, gift or package deal in chat. For a doctor-led clinic that is the compliance line we built to. If you take instalment plans on major bank cards, we write that into its knowledge and it says so when asked. For a salon or studio with its own promotions, we load whatever your price list says.
