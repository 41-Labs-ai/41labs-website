What it does inside your sale

Step by step, in your flow, not a generic chatbot script

  1. It answers the availability question. On Pro it reads your fleet and booking sheet before it names a car, offers up to three that are free for those dates with a photo and the rate, and never offers a car that is already out. If your availability lives on a whiteboard, it does not guess. It answers the rest and tells the driver a person will confirm the car.
  2. It qualifies the driver. For a private-hire driver it asks which platform he drives for, whether he holds a PDVL and a Class 3 licence, when he wants to collect and whether he wants 15, 30 or 60 weeks, or whatever terms you run. For a weekend hire it asks the dates, how many people, the car class and whether the car is going into Malaysia. One question at a time, no forms.
  3. It quotes from your rate card and holds the rate. Daily, weekly, monthly or by term, straight from the list you give us. It explains the Malaysia charge and the excess before it is asked. When the driver says his friend pays less or your ad had a promo, it keeps your figure and still moves him to the next step. If your rates are on request, it never quotes and says a person will confirm.
  4. It takes the deposit and writes the booking. On Pro it sends a payment link in the chat with the deposit shown as a hold. Once it is paid it writes the booking row and status back to your sheet and sends the collection details. On Starter it tells the driver the deposit and hands him to your admin to collect it.
  5. It follows up and hands over. A driver who goes quiet is chased after a day and again a week later, then it stops. A customer with a booking can read it back and move the dates or swap the car on the same reference. A cancellation frees the days. Corporate fleet requests, accidents, disputes and workshop complaints go to a named person on your team with the whole conversation attached.

What it connects to. On Starter it reads your vehicle list and rate card as a spreadsheet or a photo, your terms for deposit, excess, mileage and Malaysia use, and your collection details. On Pro it reads and writes your fleet and booking sheet in Google Sheets, sends a payment link and alerts your admin on every handover. We move your availability into Google Sheets for you. A fleet system or a CRM of your own we look at first and price before we start.

AI agent for car rental companies: questions owners ask

Can an AI agent rent cars to Grab and Gojek drivers over WhatsApp?

Yes, and it is the flow we have built most often. The driver asks if there is a car, and the agent asks which platform he drives for, whether he holds a PDVL and a Class 3 licence, when he wants to start and for how long. It quotes from your rate card, or if your rates are on request, tells him a person will confirm. Then it hands the qualified driver to your admin, or on Pro takes the deposit in the chat.

Can it tell a customer whether a car is available this weekend?

On Pro, yes. It reads your fleet and booking sheet in Google Sheets and checks the dates before it names a car, so it never offers one that is already out. It replies to an inbound message at any hour, and its own follow up nudges stay inside the hours you set. On Starter it does not see availability. It quotes and collects the details, and a person confirms the car.

What happens when a driver haggles on the rate?

It holds your rate. A driver who says his friend pays less, or that your ad had a promo, gets the same figure and a reason to go ahead. It does not invent discounts and it does not drop the price to close. If you do run a promotion, we load it and it quotes that.

Can it take the deposit?

On Pro it sends a payment link in the chat and shows the deposit as a hold. Once the payment is in, it writes the booking row to your sheet and sends the collection details. You set a ceiling it can confirm on its own, and anything above that waits for a person. If you take PayNow, it can send your details and your admin confirms the transfer, because PayNow cannot confirm itself in the chat.

What if a customer wants to change dates, swap the car or cancel?

It reads the booking back as your sheet shows it, with the reference, the car, the dates and whether it is paid or held. A date move or a car swap keeps the same reference and charges only the difference. A cancellation gets a clear yes first, then frees the days in the sheet. It never promises a refund on its own, that goes to your team.

I do not have a fixed rate card. Can it still work?

Yes. We set it to never quote a figure and never name a car. It answers the rest and collects platform, licence, start date and term. Then it tells the driver a person will come back with the rate. One operator with over 200 vehicles runs it this way.
