What it does inside your sale

Step by step, in your flow, not a generic chatbot script

  1. It answers the slot or delivery question first. A groomer's customer asks for Saturday. The agent reads your calendar and offers the open slots. A pet food customer asks to move to Thursday. The agent knows the delivery day for each area and the windows you run, and takes the window.
  2. It quotes from your price list, by breed and size or by bowl and weight. Your grooming menu with the small, medium and large prices, or your product list with the 200g and 500g prices, is what it quotes from. For fresh food it also reads your feeding guide and tells a customer how much a 10kg dog eats in a day.
  3. It asks the one question that changes the answer. Breed and size before a grooming price. Whether someone will be home before a fresh food delivery. Which window before it confirms. One question at a time, the way you would ask it.
  4. It books, moves or changes, then confirms. On Pro it writes the slot into your calendar, moves the delivery in your sheet or WooCommerce, splits the subscription into two deliveries a week and sends the payment link for an added item. On Starter it confirms the request with the customer and sends it to you to key in. Either way the customer gets one clear confirmation message.
  5. It follows up and hands over. A quiet enquiry gets a nudge. A subscriber gets a stock reminder or a renewal reminder through an approved WhatsApp template. A complaint or a refund request goes to you with the order number, and so does a question about a dog's health condition. The agent does not answer those.

What it connects to. On Starter it reads what you send us: your grooming menu, product list, feeding guide, delivery policy and calendar link. Prices and stock change when you tell us. On Pro it reads your booking calendar and writes the slot in, and it reads WooCommerce or your delivery spreadsheet for live stock, the next delivery date and the subscription, then writes the change back. It sends a card or PayNow payment link in the chat. Shopee chat stays inside Shopee, so it cannot reach those buyers.

Then this agent will not reach those buyers. "Complaints need a person. I do not want an AI arguing with a customer over a bag of food."

It will not argue. A complaint is a handover: the agent apologises once, takes the order number and sends it to you. It offers no refund and makes no excuse. You reply from your own phone and the agent stays out of that thread.

"I am one groomer with one table. Is S$690 a month worth it for me?"

Count the bookings you lose. If enquiries are few and you answer them within minutes from the table, a WhatsApp Business auto-reply and a booking link will do, and you should keep your money. No. On Pro it reads the subscription from WooCommerce and writes the delivery change or the added item back, so WooCommerce stays the one place your orders live. On Starter it does not touch WooCommerce at all. It tells you what the customer wants changed and you key it in.

AI agent for pet shops and groomers: questions owners ask

Can an AI agent take grooming bookings on WhatsApp?

Yes. The customer asks for Saturday, the agent asks the breed and size, quotes from your menu and offers the open slots. On Pro it writes the booking into your calendar and sends the confirmation. On Starter it confirms with the customer and sends you the booking to key in. It answers in English and the mixed English Singapore customers type.

Can it change a customer's delivery day or subscription?

Yes. It knows which day you deliver to each area and which windows you run, so it can move a delivery to Thursday evening and remind the customer that someone must be home. It can switch a plan to two deliveries a week. On Pro the change is written into WooCommerce or your delivery sheet. On Starter you get the request and make the change yourself.

Can it tell a customer whether something is in stock?

On Starter it answers from the product list you give us, and you tell us when something sells out. On Pro it reads live stock from WooCommerce, so the answer is right the moment the customer asks. If a product is out, it takes the reminder request, and the back in stock message goes out through an approved template when you tell us it has returned.

Can it send stock reminders and subscription reminders to past customers?

Yes, through WhatsApp message templates that Meta approves first. Approval takes time, so we submit the templates during the build. It sends to customers who gave you their WhatsApp number through your website, your shop or an event. It cannot send to Shopee buyers, because Shopee hides the buyer's details.

Does it work with Shopee or Lazada chat?

No. Shopee keeps its chat inside the app, and no outside tool can reply there. 41 Closer runs on your WhatsApp number and, if you want, your website. If most of your customers buy on marketplaces, start by moving them to your WhatsApp with a card in the parcel, and then the agent has someone to talk to.

Do I need a new WhatsApp number for the agent?

We recommend one. The agent runs on the WhatsApp Business API, and past chats on your current number do not carry over to it. Your existing number keeps working on your phone. You put the new number on your website, your packaging and your booking page, and the agent answers there. If you want it on your current number instead, ask us first, because connecting it changes how that number works.
