What it does inside your sale

Step by step, in your flow, not a generic chatbot script

  1. It answers the first message and places the child. A parent writes 'do you teach at condo pools? my son is 5' and gets an answer in the same reply, not a request for their name. It recommends one programme by age (2 to 3 toddler, 4 to 12 kids, 13 to 17 teenager, adults, seniors) and says the coach checks the level in the first lesson. It never assigns a level or promises how many lessons it takes.
  2. It collects the five details a coach match needs. Who is learning and how old, which condo or area, which days and time window, female or male coach, private or group. One question per message, skipping anything the parent already said. This replaces the Google Form one owner sends after the first message and then reads back by hand.
  3. It quotes from your price list and answers the policy questions. Wet weather, cancellation notice, make-up lessons, two siblings in one group, certificates, all answered from the facts you give us. If you publish no prices, it says your admin sends the fee and never guesses. A parent who asks for a discount gets your price, the same every time.
  4. It offers the trial and sends you the request. A single trial lesson before any package. It asks for the name, the condo and the contact number in one message, recaps the request to the parent, and sends you everything a coach needs on WhatsApp. If the parent goes quiet after the trial offer, it checks in after three hours and again the next day.
  5. It hands over what it should not touch. An existing student's reschedule, an invoice question or a complaint about a coach goes to you with the student's name and the lesson time, after one line of acknowledgement. Someone trying to hire your coach privately is declined politely. It never says a slot is booked or a coach is confirmed when it cannot see your roster.

What it connects to. On Starter it reads the programme list, price list, areas and policies you give us, and hands every trial request to you on WhatsApp. On Pro it can read your booking calendar or coach availability sheet, write the trial booking back and take the trial payment in the chat. One owner asked for it to text each coach for a yes or no. That is not built, so ask us before you count on it.
