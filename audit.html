What is the 41 Labs AI Readiness Audit?

Using our proprietary 4+1 Method — Audit, Adopt, Automate, Amplify, plus your Quick Win — we evaluate your company across five dimensions: digital infrastructure, data readiness, process maturity, team capability, and innovation culture.
