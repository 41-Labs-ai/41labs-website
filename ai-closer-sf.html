Questions

What owners ask us first.

Is the demo a sales pitch?

Partly, and we'll be upfront about it. You get the leak audit and a live demo either way. If the Closer fits your business, we'll tell you what it costs on the call. If it doesn't, we'll say so and you keep the audit.

Is this a chatbot?

No. A chatbot answers questions and sends menus. 41 Closer sells. It qualifies the buyer, recommends, quotes from your prices, follows up, and books the sale or takes the deposit.

What if it says something wrong?

It only works from what you give it: your prices, stock and policies. When it isn't sure, it hands the chat to your team with the full history. Your team tests it for two weeks before any customer sees it.

How long does it take?

Six weeks from go-ahead. We build it in the first two, your team tests it in weeks 3 and 4, and it goes live in phases after that.

What does it cost?

It depends on your chat volume, your systems and what the Closer needs to do. There's a one-time build, then a monthly fee once it's live that covers us running and improving it. On the call we look at your numbers first and give you an exact figure. If it won't pay for itself, we'll tell you.

Who runs it day to day?

We do. We watch it, tune it and report the results to you every week. Your team steps in when a customer needs a person.

Does it use my WhatsApp?

Yes. It runs on the WhatsApp Business API under your own business account, and that account stays yours.
