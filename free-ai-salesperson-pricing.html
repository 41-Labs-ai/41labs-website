Billing questions.

Is there a contract?

No. Your first 100 sales conversations are free, then it is month to month. Stop any time with one message.

When do I start paying?

After your free 100 sales conversations. If you use them early, we ask first and charge nothing without your yes. If you have not used them by day 30, your plan starts on day 31. Your card is saved on the call, and we message you a week before.

Can I pay yearly?

Yes, on Starter and above. Pay for 10 months and get 12. It is optional. Start is monthly only.

Can I change plans?

Yes. Tell us on WhatsApp and we change it with you. If Start would cost you more than Starter in a month, we move you to Starter.

Who pays WhatsApp's message fees?

Meta bills them to your own WhatsApp Business account. They are separate from your plan.

Do you charge GST?

No.

Can you guarantee results?

No. Your first 100 sales conversations are free, so you see it work on your own customers before you pay anything.
