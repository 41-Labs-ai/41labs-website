How can I automate my WhatsApp?

There are three ways to automate WhatsApp, and they sit at three price points. The right one depends on two things: how much money a WhatsApp enquiry is worth to you, and who on your team will actually run the system.

Option 1: The free built-in tools

Download the WhatsApp Business app and open Business tools. You can set a greeting message that fires when someone messages you for the first time, an away message for after hours, and quick replies you trigger with a shortcut. Setup takes ten minutes and costs $0.

Is WhatsApp automation legal in Singapore?

Yes. Automation on the official WhatsApp Business API is fully permitted by Meta. This is a mainstream, sanctioned way to do business. Meta has reported that more than 200 million businesses use its WhatsApp Business tools every month. Two sets of rules apply, and both are simple to follow.

Start with Meta's side. Customers must opt in before you send them business-initiated messages, for example by messaging you first, ticking a box on your website, or agreeing in person. Once a customer messages you, you can reply freely inside a 24-hour service window. Outside that window, you send pre-approved template messages.

Singapore adds its own layer. The Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) requires consent to collect and use personal data, and you must honour requests to withdraw it. Before sending marketing messages to Singapore numbers without clear consent, check the Do Not Call Registry. The Spam Control Act adds the basics for bulk commercial messages: identify your business accurately and include a working unsubscribe option. If you only message people who contacted you first, you are on the safest possible ground.

The illegal, or at least ban-worthy, corner is unofficial mods and scripts that automate a personal WhatsApp account. Meta detects and bans those numbers. Any serious tool, DIY or managed, runs on the official API.

Is WhatsApp automation free?

Partly. The free tier is real, and so is the bill once you outgrow it.

The WhatsApp Business app costs nothing, and its greeting messages, away messages, and quick replies are genuine automation. If all you need is "thanks, we'll reply in the morning", stop there and pay nobody.

Paid DIY platforms are the next layer, from about US$29 to US$79 a month at entry level, and annual billing is usually required for the lowest price. On top of that, Meta bills per-message fees for business-initiated template messages, with marketing templates costing the most. Replies you send inside the 24-hour customer service window carry no Meta fee, which is why automation that responds to inbound enquiries is the cheapest kind to run.
