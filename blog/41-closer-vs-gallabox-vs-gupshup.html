Self-serve platform or done-for-you agent

Gallabox starts affordable. Gupshup runs at enterprise scale. 41 Closer starts at S$690 a month. If price were the whole story, this article would be one line long.

Here is what the pricing pages do not show. A self-serve platform is a box of parts. Someone in your company has to connect the WhatsApp Business API, build the AI agents and campaigns, write every reply, watch the inbox daily, and fix what breaks. Then keep improving it every month, because the first version is never right.

If you have that person, self-serve is genuinely good value. Gallabox and Gupshup both give you a lot of capability for the money, and between them they handle billions of conversations.

If you do not have that person, a familiar pattern plays out. The tool gets half set up over a free trial. The agent learns to answer three FAQs. Then a customer asks a real question, the agent has no answer, and your staff go back to typing replies by hand. The subscription keeps billing. Nobody cancels it, and nobody uses it.

That is not a flaw in Gallabox or Gupshup. It is the adoption pattern of every self-serve tool, from CRMs to email software. A done-for-you agent flips the model. 41 Labs builds it, runs it, and tunes it, and you review results instead of building flows. So the honest question is not which platform is best. It is who in your company will run it.

What is Gallabox?

Gallabox is a self-serve WhatsApp commerce platform built on the official WhatsApp Business API. Small and growing teams use it to run a shared inbox, no-code chatbot flows, broadcasts, catalog, and payments, plus no-code AI agents on WhatsApp and Instagram. It says it has handled 2 billion conversations across 40 countries. Plans start from ₹2,999 a month.

What is Gupshup?

Gupshup is a large conversational messaging platform, often called a CPaaS, built for scale. Businesses and developers use its Conversation Cloud to run AI agents, campaigns, and support across WhatsApp, Instagram, SMS, RCS, and voice, powered by its own ACE LLM. It says it handles 120 billion conversations a year for 50,000+ customers in 60+ countries.

What is 41 Closer?

41 Closer is a managed AI sales agent for WhatsApp, built and run by 41 Labs in Singapore. It answers every enquiry in seconds, 24/7, quotes real prices from your own catalogue, follows up every lead, and closes the sale. There is no software for your team to learn. Plans start at S$690 a month.

When Gallabox or Gupshup is the better choice

Plenty of businesses should buy the platform, not the service. Be honest with yourself about which list you are on.

  • You want to start affordable and run it yourself. Gallabox has a Basic plan from ₹2,999 a month billed quarterly, about US$120, and a free trial. 41 Closer starts at S$690 a month and cannot match that entry price, and I will not pretend otherwise.
  • You want WhatsApp sales, marketing, and support in one tool. Gallabox combines a shared inbox, broadcasts, catalog, payments, and no-code AI agents, so a small team can run the whole thing from one login.
  • You operate at large scale or across many channels. Gupshup is built for volume. It says it handles 120 billion conversations a year across WhatsApp, Instagram, SMS, RCS, and voice, with enterprise guardrails and its own ACE LLM.
  • You have developers who want APIs. Gupshup is a CPaaS, so your engineers can build directly on its messaging infrastructure. Gallabox also offers integrations for a technical team to wire up.
  • You have a capable person to run it. Someone who enjoys building flows and agents, testing them, and improving them monthly. In those hands, both platforms return far more than they cost.
  • You want to keep everything in-house. Self-serve means you own the account, the data, and the day-to-day, with no outside team involved.

If two or more of those describe you, buy the platform. It is the right call, and you will not miss what 41 Closer does. For more options, see our guide to the best WhatsApp AI tools in Singapore.

Questions to ask before you choose

Run any WhatsApp AI option, including 41 Closer, through this short checklist before you sign anything.

  • Who on my team will connect the WhatsApp Business API and keep it running? If the honest answer is nobody, a self-serve platform will sit unused.
  • Which plan does the AI actually switch on? On Gallabox, AI agents start on the Essential plan, not the entry Basic plan, so price the tier you actually need.
  • Is the pricing published in a currency I can budget in? Gallabox is priced in Indian rupees, and Gupshup does not publish fixed tiers, so confirm the real figure before you commit.
  • What will WhatsApp per-conversation charges add on top? These are billed separately from the software on every platform here.
  • How fast do we answer enquiries today, and what does a slow reply cost us? Put a dollar figure on the leads that go cold after hours.
  • Do we need many channels and scale, or enquiries answered and closed? The first points to Gupshup or Gallabox. The second points to a managed agent.
  • If it breaks or the agents go stale, who fixes it, us or the vendor? With self-serve it is you. With a managed service it is the provider.

What is Gupshup used for?

Gupshup is a large conversational messaging platform, often called a CPaaS. Businesses and developers use it to send and automate messages across WhatsApp, Instagram, SMS, RCS, and voice, run click-to-chat ad campaigns, and build AI agents on its Conversation Cloud using its own ACE LLM. It says it handles 120 billion conversations a year for 50,000+ customers in 60+ countries, including large brands. It is built for scale and for teams that have people to run it, so it suits larger or high-volume businesses more than a small shop that just wants enquiries answered.
