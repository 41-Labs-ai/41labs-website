AI development company in Singapore: questions buyers ask

What is the best AI development company in Singapore?

It depends on what you want built and who will run it afterwards. For a custom system scoped from scratch, Singapore studios such as Olano and DoubleAM scope each build individually and publish no price list. For a platform your own team configures, Voltade, Pepper Cloud and SleekFlow are Singapore-priced options. 41 Labs is the right choice when the job is selling over WhatsApp and you want it built on your prices, connected to your systems and run for you for a monthly fee. We publish a comparison with their prices and the date we checked.

How much does it cost to build a custom AI system in Singapore?

Studios that scope custom builds from scratch do not publish prices, so the honest answer is that it depends on the scope and you get a quote. 41 Labs no longer sells project builds. 41 Closer is S$690 a month on Starter and S$1,490 on Pro, with the build included and no setup fee. Connecting a system of your own is a one-time S$2,400 for a platform we have connected before. No GST is charged. Eligible Singapore SMEs can apply the EDGE grant, which replaced the EDG on 30 September 2026, to part of the cost.

Will the AI be trained on our own business data?

Yes. The agent is built on your product list, prices, rules and the way you sell, and on Pro it reads your live systems rather than a copy. You own the agent and everything it learns. We never share or sell your data. It answers only from what you gave it, so it does not invent a price.

How long does it take to develop and go live?

48 hours from the day you send your product list and prices for the agent itself. Connecting your own stock, booking or pricing system adds time, scoped per system. Before you pay, we build your agent and you message it, then one free hour to see the platform and connect payments and your WhatsApp number.

Do you develop anything other than the sales agent?

Only the connections around it: your systems into the agent and the agent's orders and bookings back into your systems. We do not take standalone custom builds, document-processing projects or consulting engagements any more. If that is what you need we will say so on the first message and point you to someone who does it.

Can we use the EDGE grant, formerly EDG, for AI development?

The EDG closed on 29 September 2026. Its replacement, the EDGE grant, covers up to 70% of qualifying costs for eligible SMEs and up to 50% for larger companies. We run the eligibility check and help with the paperwork. It does not change the monthly price or the no-contract terms.
