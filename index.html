Frequently asked questions

About 41 Sales Team

What is 41 Sales Team?

41 Sales Team (formerly 41 Closer) is an AI sales team on your WhatsApp, built and run by 41 Labs in Singapore. The AI Sales Closer answers every enquiry, quotes, books and sends the payment link. The AI Operations Admin handles everything after payment. The AI Manager tells you what happened and what to fix. The AI Leads Generator is coming soon.

Does my staff still reply?

Yes. The AI works with your staff. Anyone on your team can step into a chat and reply at any time.

Do my customers see a new number?

No. Your customers keep messaging your same WhatsApp number.

What languages does it reply in?

It replies in your customer's language. If they write in Chinese, it answers in Chinese.

What if I want to stop?

It is month to month with 30 days notice and no contract. If you leave, we export your data and hand it to you.

What do I need to prepare?

Nothing. We build it from your website before we meet. On the call we connect your WhatsApp. You have no keys or accounts to manage.

Custom AI projects with 41 Labs

How is this different from ChatGPT or other AI tools?

Generic AI tools work with generic data. We build systems specifically trained on your internal information: your products, your processes, your historical data. The result is accuracy you can actually trust for business decisions, not just impressive-sounding responses.

Is my data secure with 41 Labs?

Yes. We build systems with enterprise-grade security. Your data stays within your infrastructure where possible, and we follow strict data handling protocols including PDPA and GDPR compliance. We're happy to walk through our security approach on a call.

How long does a typical AI project take?

Most projects go from kickoff to production in 4-8 weeks, depending on complexity. We scope carefully upfront so there are no surprises. We'd rather ship something focused that works than a bloated system that doesn't.

What does 41 Labs pricing look like?

We typically work on project-based pricing scoped after our discovery call. No hourly billing that drags on forever. We'll give you a clear proposal with defined deliverables and timeline before any commitment. For typical ranges, see what AI consulting costs in Singapore.

Do you offer ongoing AI support?

Yes. After deployment, we offer maintenance and iteration packages to keep your system running smoothly and improving over time. We also train your team so you're not dependent on us for day-to-day operations.

Is there government funding available for AI automation in Singapore?

Yes. Singapore's EDGE grant (formerly the Enterprise Development Grant, EDG) covers up to 50% of qualifying AI and innovation projects (up to 70% for qualifying SMEs). Eligible businesses must be registered and operating in Singapore with at least 30% local shareholding. 41 Labs helps clients with the application process. The EDGE grant is administered by Enterprise Singapore.

What if I'm not sure AI is right for my problem?

That's exactly what the discovery call is for. We'll be honest about whether AI is the right solution, and if it's not, we'll tell you. We'd rather turn down a project than build something that won't deliver value.
