Frequently asked questions

Will it sound like a bot?

No. We train it on your products, your prices and how you talk to customers, and we keep tuning it. You test it yourself before it goes live.

Do customers need a new number?

No. It works on your existing WhatsApp Business number. Your customers notice faster replies, nothing else.

Can my staff still reply?

Yes. Anyone on your team can step into a chat at any time. The AI waits, then picks up when they finish.

What if it cannot answer?

It hands the chat to the right person on your team and tells them on WhatsApp. It never makes up an answer.

What languages does it reply in?

Your customer's language. If they write in Chinese, it answers in Chinese.

What do I need to prepare?

Your website. A price list or a voice note helps. We do the rest.

How long does setup take?

We build it before the call. If you like it on the call, it goes live the same day.

What does it cost?

Plans start small and grow with the number of conversations. See pricing, or we work out the right plan with you on the call.

Is my data safe?

We follow PDPA. If you leave, we export your data and hand it to you.
