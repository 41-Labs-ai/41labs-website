The real reason WhatsApp enquiries go cold

It's rarely the price. A customer who messages you is ready to talk right now. What happens in the next few minutes decides it.

01 · SPEED

Whoever replies first usually wins

The customer who messages at 11pm has messaged two other businesses too. The first real answer gets the booking.

02 · CERTAINTY

"Let me check" loses to a clear answer

A reply with a real price, a real slot and a next step lets them say yes in the chat. A vague reply sends them back to shopping.

03 · FOLLOW-UP

Quiet isn't the same as no

Plenty of buyers go quiet because life got busy. One polite follow-up at the right time brings a lot of them back.

And this is where the Closer makes you money

  1. Nights, weekends and public holidays.Every enquiry gets a proper answer in seconds, so the 11pm customer books with you, not the next business on their list.
  2. Quotes and bookings inside the chat.It works from your own price list, stock and calendar, so the customer can say yes without waiting for your team.
  3. Every open quote followed up.It chases politely until they answer, and hands your team a warm lead with the whole conversation, not a cold "hi".

Frequently asked questions

What happens on the free demo?

20 minutes on Google Meet with Alexander. We show you your leak audit, work out what your unanswered enquiries are worth, and show you the Closer handling an enquiry like the ones your business gets.

What's the WhatsApp leak audit?

Before the call, we send your business a real enquiry the way a customer would. We time the first reply, check what happens after hours, and see whether anyone follows up. You'll see the results on the call.

Is the demo a sales pitch?

Partly, and we'll be upfront about it. You get the audit and the demo either way. If the Closer fits your business, we'll tell you what it costs. If it doesn't, we'll say so.

Is this a chatbot?

No. A chatbot answers questions and sends menus. 41 Closer sells. It qualifies the buyer, recommends, quotes from your prices, follows up, and books the sale or takes the deposit.

Will it work for my business?

If your customers ask, get quotes, order or book on WhatsApp, very likely. That's what the demo is for. We'll look at your chats and your numbers and tell you honestly.

What if it says something wrong?

It only works from what you give it: your prices, stock and policies. When it isn't sure, it hands the chat to your team with the full history. Your team tests it for two weeks before any customer sees it.

How long does it take to go live?

Six weeks from go-ahead. We build it in the first two, your team tests it in weeks 3 and 4, and it goes live in phases after that.

What does it cost?

It depends on your chat volume, your systems and what the Closer needs to do. There's a one-time build, then a monthly fee once it's live that covers us running it. On the demo we look at your numbers first and give you an exact figure. If it won't pay for itself, we'll tell you.

Does it use my WhatsApp number?

Yes. It runs on the WhatsApp Business API under your own business account, and that account stays yours.

Who runs it day to day?

We do. We watch it, tune it and send you the numbers every week. Your team steps in when a customer needs a person.

Can I bring my sales manager to the demo?

Please do. It helps to have whoever answers your WhatsApp today on the call.
