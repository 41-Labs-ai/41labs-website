How Plato booking works

How your AI books with Plato

At the end of this guide, you know what your AI does with each booking, which rules it keeps, and how to test it before your patients use it.

This guide starts where Connecting your Plato account ends, at docs.41labs.ai/hermes/connect-plato. Plato must be connected and show Working.

Before you start

You need:

  • Your 41 Sales Team dashboard, with the owner or admin role.
  • The Plato page open, under Settings → Connections → Plato.
  • Your Plato diary open, to check what your AI writes.
  • A test phone that is not set up as an admin number on your dashboard, and whose number is not in Plato yet.
  • About 20 minutes for the test in Step by step.

One booking from start to finish

Every booking goes through the same eight stages. Your AI never invents a time. Every time it offers comes from your Plato diary and passes your rules.

One booking from the first message to the reply, in eight stages.

What the patient hears at the end depends on Who books, below. In Plato, an appointment your AI makes carries the title Booked on WhatsApp.

Who books

On the Plato page, Who books sets what happens when a patient picks a time. The default is Your AI books, your team confirms. A change saves at once.

Under Your AI books, your team confirms, the appointment sits in Plato as not yet confirmed (the status Scheduled) until your team confirms it there.

How your AI finds free times

Which daysToday and the next 6 days, or the day the patient asked for. Never more than 14 days in one look, and never past the furthest booking day.
Which calendarsOnly the calendars switched on. Only days you are open that are not closed dates.
Which timesPlato's free times on each calendar, in steps of the appointment length. Each one is checked against every rule above.
How manyUp to three. The first free time of the morning, the afternoon and the evening of the earliest day, then times at least two hours apart. The same time on two calendars is one offer that names both.
Nothing freeYour AI says so and asks for other days or another time of day. It never invents a time.
When the patient picksThe rules are checked again, Plato is asked whether the time is still free, then it is written or handed to your team. The notice counts from when the times were offered, so a patient who answers after a few minutes is not refused.
Old offersA time offered more than a day ago, or now less than 15 minutes away, is not booked. Your AI offers fresh times.
Time just takenWhen Plato says the time is no longer free, nothing is booked. Your AI offers fresh times.
Plato does not answerThe patient hears your team will confirm a time. Your team gets a WhatsApp alert. After three failures in a row, the Plato page shows Needs you.

How your AI knows who the patient is

Your AI looks up the patient in Plato by the last 8 digits of the WhatsApp number. What it does next depends on how many patients it finds.

How your AI decides who the patient is: none, one, or more than one patient in Plato with this phone number.

One patientBefore any booking or move, your AI asks whether the appointment is for that patient, by first name only. It says nothing else from the record. A yes books. A no, or another name, goes to your team. The question is good for 30 minutes.
New patientYour AI asks for the full name as on the IC or your clinic records, at least two words. It never uses the WhatsApp profile name, which is often a nickname. The record holds the name and the phone only.
Shared phoneYour AI lists the first names on that number and asks which person it is for. It then lists, moves or cancels that person's appointments only. A booking on a shared phone goes to your team.
Own appointments onlyA patient sees, moves or cancels only appointments under their own WhatsApp number. Up to five upcoming appointments are listed, without any record details.
Never askedAn NRIC. Your clinic collects it at the visit.

What your AI can see and change, on the Plato page.

Moving and cancelling

A patient can ask to move or cancel an appointment in the chat. Your AI first finds the appointment under their number, then follows the rules you set.

What happens when a patient asks to move or cancel: a late change or Your team books it goes to your team, otherwise your AI does it.

A moveYour AI offers free times as for a booking. When the patient picks one, it confirms the patient by first name, checks the rules again, checks the time is still free in Plato, then moves it.
A cancelThe appointment is set to cancelled in Plato. The record stays. The patient hears it is cancelled.
A late changeInside the late-change window, your team handles it. The patient hears your team will contact them. Nothing is changed.
Already cancelledYour AI says so and offers to book a new time.
Already startedYour AI changes nothing and hands it to your team.

When a person takes over

A hand-over always looks the same to the patient and to your team. Every Plato alert reaches your team, even a second one within ten minutes.

A hand-over: your AI stops, the chat shows under Needs your input, your team is alerted, a person replies.

Step by step: test it before your patients use it

The test uses a real phone and a made-up test patient, so Plato shows exactly what your patients will get. Tidy up in Plato at the end.

The safe test before patients use it, in six stages.

01

Prepare a test phone

  1. Pick a phone whose number is not set as an admin number on your dashboard.
  2. Check in Plato that no patient has that number.
  3. On the Plato page, set Who books and the hand-over rules the way you want them live. Leave The patient is new off for this test.
02

Book as a new patient

  1. From the test phone, message your business WhatsApp number and ask for an appointment more than two days away.
  2. Pick one of the up to three times your AI offers.
  3. When your AI asks for the full name, type a made-up two-word name, such as Test Patient.
  4. Check Plato: a patient with that name and the test phone number, and the appointment on the calendar you named, titled Booked on WhatsApp.
  5. Check the status: not yet confirmed under Your AI books, your team confirms, or confirmed under Your AI books it.
  6. Check your team's WhatsApp for the alert, if you chose a team step.
03

Book again as a known patient

  1. From the same phone, ask for another appointment.
  2. Pick a time. Your AI asks whether the appointment is for Test, the first name only.
  3. Answer yes.
  4. Check Plato: the second appointment is under the same patient.

Answer no instead, and your AI books nothing and hands the chat to your team. Try it once if you want to see a hand-over.

04

Move it

  1. From the test phone, ask to move the first appointment.
  2. Pick one of the times your AI offers, more than 24 hours away.
  3. Answer yes when your AI asks whether it is for Test.
  4. Check Plato: the appointment moved to the new time.
05

Try a late change

  1. From the test phone, ask for a time today or tomorrow, inside your late-change window, and book it.
  2. Ask to cancel it.
  3. Check the chat: your AI says your team will contact you, and changes nothing.
  4. Check your inbox: the chat shows under Needs your input.
  5. Check your team's WhatsApp for the urgent alert.
06

Cancel and tidy up

  1. From the test phone, ask to cancel the appointment that is more than 24 hours away. Check Plato: it is cancelled.
  2. In Plato, cancel the remaining test appointment.
  3. In Plato, delete the test patient record.
  4. On the Plato page, set The patient is new the way you want it live.

Checklist

What your AI does not do

  • Different hours for each doctor or room. The opening hours apply to every calendar.
  • Different lengths for different treatments. Every appointment uses the one length.
  • A gap between two appointments.
  • Holding a time between the offer and the booking. When someone takes it in between, your AI says so and offers fresh times.
  • Telling the patient when your clinic moves or cancels an appointment in Plato.
  • Asking for an NRIC, or showing a patient anything from another patient's record.

Good to know

  • Setting up the connection, the hours and the limits: Connecting your Plato account at docs.41labs.ai/hermes/connect-plato.
  • In a test conversation on your dashboard, nothing is written to Plato and nobody is alerted.
  • When Plato stops accepting the key, the Plato page shows Needs you, and your AI hands every booking to your team until you connect again.
