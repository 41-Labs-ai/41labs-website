Connecting your Plato booking rules

At the end of this guide, every booking rule on your Plato page is set and tested from your own phone.

This guide starts where Connecting your Plato account ends, at docs.41labs.ai/hermes/connect-plato. The Plato page must show Working.

Before you start

You need:

  • Your dashboard, with the owner or admin role.
  • The Plato page open, under Settings → Connections → Plato.
  • Your Plato diary and patient list open.
  • A test phone that is not set up as an admin number on your dashboard, and whose number is not in Plato yet.
  • About 25 minutes.

What happens in this guide

Steps 1 to 6 are on the Plato page. Steps 7 to 15 use your test phone, your Plato diary and your dashboard.

The four stages of this guide, with the steps each one covers.

Step by step

01

Choose who books

  1. On the Plato page, under Who books, pick one of the three choices.

The change saves at once. What you will see for each choice:

The three Who books choices, row by row.

Under Your AI books, your team confirms, the appointment shows in Plato with the status Scheduled until your team confirms it there.

02

Choose when a person steps in

  1. Under Hand to a person when, switch on The patient is new if your team creates every new patient record.
  2. Under A change or cancel comes in late, pick 12, 24 or 48 hours.

What you will see at every hand-over:

A hand-over, from your AI's last message to a person's reply.

Your team also gets an alert, and the patient hears your team will be in touch, when:

  • Who books is Your team books it.
  • A booking comes from a phone with more than one patient in Plato.
  • The patient says the appointment is not for <first name>.
  • The patient record in Plato has no name, or a name your AI cannot safely shorten to a first name, such as a two-word name with the surname last.
  • The appointment has already started or passed.
  • Plato does not answer, or refuses the change.

A second alert about the same thing within ten minutes is held back.

03

Check the calendars your AI may book

  1. Under Calendars, read each name.
  2. Switch off a calendar your AI must not book.
  3. Check the badge counts the bookable calendars.

A switch saves at once. Your AI offers and books on the switched-on calendars only.

04

Check the opening hours

  1. Under Opening hours, check the opening and closing time of each day.
  2. Type a Last start for a day, or leave it empty.
  3. Switch on Lunch for a day and set its start and end.
  4. Under Each appointment, pick 15, 20, 30, 45, 60, 90 or 120 minutes.
  5. Press Save hours.

What you will see your AI offer on such a day:

A day with lunch, a last start and 2 hours of notice: the green times are offered.

05

Set the booking limits

  1. Under Earliest new booking, pick 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12, 24, 48 or 72 hours.
  2. Under Furthest booking, pick 7, 14, 30, 60, 90 or 180 days.
  3. Under Closed dates, add each public holiday or day off, up to 60.
  4. Press Save limits.

Past closed dates drop off when you save. What you will see:

Furthest booking 30 days and two closed dates: the green days are offered.

06

Read what your AI can see and change

  1. Under What your AI can see and change, read the It reads list.
  2. Read the It changes list.

The test, Steps 7 to 15, uses a real phone and a made-up test patient. Tidy up in Plato at the end.

The test before patients use it, in six stages.

07

Prepare a test phone

  1. Pick a phone whose number is not an admin number on your dashboard.
  2. In Plato, search the patient list for that number.
  3. Check that no patient has it.
  4. On the Plato page, set Who books and the hand-over rules the way you want them live.
  5. Switch The patient is new off for this test.

A test conversation on your dashboard writes nothing to Plato and alerts nobody. Use the real phone.

Screenshot to come: the Plato patient list, searched by the test number, with no result.

08

Book as a new patient

  1. From the test phone, message your business WhatsApp number.
  2. Ask for an appointment more than two days away.
  3. Pick one of the times your AI offers.
  4. When your AI asks for the full name, type a made-up two-word name, such as Test Patient.

What you will see in the chat:

A new patient: your AI asks for the full name before it books.

09

Find the booking in Plato

  1. In the Plato patient list, find a patient with that name and the test number.
  2. In the Plato diary, find the appointment on the calendar you named, titled Booked on WhatsApp.
  3. Check its status: Scheduled under Your AI books, your team confirms, or confirmed under Your AI books it.
  4. Check your team's WhatsApp for the alert, if you chose a team step.

Your AI checked the time again before it wrote it:

What your AI checks after the patient picks a time.

Screenshot to come: the Plato diary with the test appointment, titled Booked on WhatsApp, on the calendar you named.

Screenshot to come: the WhatsApp alert your team gets, with the test name and the time.

10

Book again as a known patient

  1. From the same phone, ask for another appointment.
  2. Pick a time.
  3. When your AI asks whether the appointment is for Test, answer yes.
  4. In the Plato diary, find the second appointment under the same patient.

What you will see in the chat:

A known patient: your AI confirms by first name only, then books.

Screenshot to come: the Plato diary with the second appointment under the same patient.

11

See the hand-over after a no

  1. From the same phone, ask for a third appointment.
  2. Pick a time.
  3. When your AI asks whether the appointment is for Test, answer no.
  4. Check the chat: your AI books nothing and says your team will be in touch.
  5. Check your team's WhatsApp for the alert.

What you will see depends on how many patients have the number:

What your AI does for none, one, or more than one patient on the number.

Screenshot to come: the WhatsApp alert your team gets after a no.

12

Move the appointment

  1. From the test phone, ask to move the first appointment.
  2. Pick one of the times your AI offers, more than 24 hours away.
  3. When your AI asks whether it is for Test, answer yes.
  4. In the Plato diary, find the appointment at its new time.

What happens when a patient asks to move or cancel:

What happens when a patient asks to move or cancel an appointment.

Screenshot to come: the Plato diary with the appointment at its new time.

13

See how a shared phone is handled

  1. In Plato, add a second patient with a made-up two-word name, such as Test Child, and the test number.
  2. From the test phone, ask to move your appointment.
  3. When your AI asks which person, answer Test.
  4. Pick a time.
  5. In the Plato diary, check that only the appointment for Test moved.

What you will see in the chat:

A shared phone: your AI asks which person first, then moves that person's appointment.

Screenshot to come: the Plato patient list with the two test patients on the same number.

14

A late change goes to your team

  1. From the test phone, ask for a time inside your late-change window, and book it.
  2. Ask to cancel it.
  3. Check the chat: your AI says your team will be in touch, and changes nothing.
  4. On your dashboard, open the chat: a grey Admin notified pill shows in the timeline.
  5. Check your team's WhatsApp for the urgent alert.

Screenshot to come: the chat on your dashboard with the grey Admin notified pill in the timeline.

Screenshot to come: the urgent WhatsApp alert your team gets about the late change.

15

Cancel and tidy up

  1. From the test phone, ask to cancel the appointment that is more than 24 hours away.
  2. In the Plato diary, check it is cancelled.
  3. In Plato, cancel the remaining test appointments.
  4. In Plato, delete the two test patient records.
  5. On the Plato page, set The patient is new the way you want it live.

Screenshot to come: the Plato diary with the test appointment cancelled.
