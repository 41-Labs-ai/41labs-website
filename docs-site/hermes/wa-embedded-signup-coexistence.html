Connecting WhatsApp Business app

Connecting your WhatsApp Business app number

At the end of this guide, the number you use in the WhatsApp Business app is connected to AI Sales Closer, the assistant replies on it, and you keep using the app on your phone.

You do every step in one sitting, with your phone in your hand. The Facebook window opens from AI Sales Closer on your computer. Some steps happen in the WhatsApp Business app on your phone.

Before you start

You need:

  • A Facebook account you can log in to.
  • The phone where your number is active in the WhatsApp Business app, updated to the latest version.
  • Your AI Sales Closer workspace, with access to Settings, open on a computer.
  • A browser that allows pop-up windows from the AI Sales Closer site.
  • About 15 minutes.

Step by step

01

Open the WhatsApp channel in AI Sales Closer

  1. In AI Sales Closer, go to Settings → Channels → WhatsApp.
  2. Press Keep the WhatsApp Business app, not Connect with Facebook. A Facebook window opens.
  3. Log in to Facebook in that window.

No window means your browser blocked it. Allow pop-ups for the AI Sales Closer site, and press the button again.

02

Accept the introduction

  1. Read the terms linked at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Press Continue.
03

Pick your business portfolio

The window reads Select the business assets to share with Hermes AI sales agent.

  1. Open the Business portfolio list.
  2. Select your business portfolio, or select Create a business portfolio if you have none.
  3. Press Next.

The list reads You’ve reached the limit for business portfolios? Select one of your existing portfolios.

04

Scan the QR code with the WhatsApp Business app

If the window asks for your number, type the number you use in the WhatsApp Business app, and press Next.

The window reads Import contacts and chat history, and shows a QR code.

  1. On your phone, open the WhatsApp Business app.
  2. Open the message from the official Facebook Business Account, and tap Connect.
  3. No message? Tap Menu or Settings, then Account, then Business Platform.
  4. Point the phone camera at the QR code on your computer screen.
05

Choose whether to share your chat history

The app reads Share chat history.

  1. Tap Share all chats to keep your current conversations in AI Sales Closer, or Don’t share chats.
  2. Tap Confirm.

You cannot change this choice later.

The app then reads Connected platform, with 41 Labs and Finish setting up in 41 Labs. Go back to the Facebook window on your computer.

06

Confirm your WhatsApp Business account

The window reads Confirm or edit your WhatsApp Business account.

  1. Check the WhatsApp Business account name, and edit it if needed.
  2. Select your Time zone.
  3. Press Next.
07

Review the access and confirm

The window reads Review what you’ll share with Hermes AI sales agent. It lists Business and WhatsApp Business account, and what AI Sales Closer can do with them.

Press Confirm.

08

Wait while Meta connects the account

The window reads Connecting your account. This takes a few moments. Keep the window open.

If the screen stops moving, press Retry.

On your phone, the app reads You’re connected to 41 Labs. Tap OK.

09

Finish in the Facebook window

The window reads Your account is connected to 41 Labs.

  1. To add a card for WhatsApp charges now, press Add payment method. You can also add it later in Meta.
  2. Press Finish. The window closes, and you return to AI Sales Closer.

Meta reviews your business, and gets in touch within 24 hours if there is an issue.

10

Check the connection in AI Sales Closer

The Connect WhatsApp box reads Connected!, with four green ticks:

  • Connected. The number keeps the WhatsApp Business app
  • App subscribed to your WABA
  • Contacts sync requested
  • Chat history sync requested
11

Turn off the app’s automatic replies

  1. In the WhatsApp Business app, open Business tools.
  2. Turn off Greeting message.
  3. Turn off Away message.

The assistant now answers first on this number.

12

Confirm the assistant replies

  1. From another phone, send a WhatsApp message to your connected number.
  2. Check that the assistant replies on WhatsApp.
  3. Check that the conversation shows in AI Sales Closer.

Using the app and the assistant together

You reply from the appThe assistant stops in that chat. When the customer writes after 12 hours with no reply from your team in that chat, the assistant answers again.
Every 14 daysOpen the WhatsApp Business app on the phone at least once, or Meta disconnects the number.
Other devicesDo not reply from WhatsApp for Windows or from a WearOS watch. AI Sales Closer may not see those replies, and the assistant keeps talking.

If something goes wrong

What you seeWhat to do
No window opens after Keep the WhatsApp Business appAllow pop-ups for the AI Sales Closer site, and press the button again.
No message from Facebook Business Account in the appUpdate the WhatsApp Business app. Then tap Settings → Account → Business Platform.
The QR code does not scanScan it from inside the WhatsApp Business app, not from the phone camera app.
Connecting your account does not finishPress Retry.
You closed the window before FinishAI Sales Closer still shows Keep the WhatsApp Business app. Press it, and start again from Step 1.
Fewer than four green ticks in Step 10Reply to your 41 Labs contact.
The assistant does not replyCheck Step 11. Then reply to your 41 Labs contact.
