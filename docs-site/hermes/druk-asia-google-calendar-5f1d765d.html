Connecting your Google Calendar

Share your team's calendars so Hermes can book a call when a customer asks to speak to a person.

You give access to only one account. Each team member shares their calendar with a single "bookings" account. 41 Labs connects to that one account at the end, on a short screen-share.

Before you start

  • The ability to create a Google account, or admin access to your Google Workspace.
  • Each team member who will take booked calls, able to sign in to Google Calendar on a computer.
  • A computer for each team member — the calendar sharing cannot be done in the phone app.
  • Your preferred booking windows to confirm with 41 Labs.
  • About 15 minutes.

Step by step

01

Create the "bookings" Google account

This is one Google account that will own all the call bookings. Create a dedicated one so bookings do not sit under a single person. Any normal Google or Google Workspace account works, as long as it can open Google Calendar.

  1. Go to accounts.google.com and choose Create account, then For work / my business.
  2. Or, if you use Google Workspace, add a new user in your Workspace admin.
  3. Give it a name the team will recognise, such as Bookings.
  4. Keep the login details — you will sign in during the screen-share with 41 Labs.

A dedicated bookings account is recommended. You may instead use an existing team member's Google account if you prefer.

02

Each team member shares their calendar

Each team member does this once, on a computer, signed in as themselves.

  1. Open calendar.google.com.
  2. Under My calendars on the left, hover over their own calendar, click the three dots, then Settings and sharing.
  3. Scroll to Share with specific people or groups, then Add people and groups.
  4. Enter the bookings account email from Step 1.
  5. In the permission dropdown, choose Make changes to events.
  6. Click Send.

Make changes to events is the exact level required. Hermes needs to add the booked call to the calendar, not just see free and busy time.

Screenshot to capture. Google's Share with specific people or groups dialog, with Make changes to events selected in the permission dropdown. To be captured on a real screen.

Repeat for every team member who will take booked calls.

03

Confirm the calendars arrived

Sign in as the bookings account and open Google Calendar. Under Other calendars on the left, you should see every team member's calendar listed.

If one is missing, that team member should redo Step 2.

04

Confirm the booking windows

Tell 41 Labs the slots you want to offer customers. Our proposal:

Mornings10:00 – 12:00 SGT
Afternoons15:00 – 17:00 SGT
DaysMonday to Friday
Call length30 minutes

Please confirm these, or tell us your preferred windows.
