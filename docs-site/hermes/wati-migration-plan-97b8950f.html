Connecting WhatsApp from WATI

Connecting your WhatsApp number from WATI

Move your WhatsApp number from WATI to Hermes in four steps, on the same number, and switch the AI on when you are ready.

The AI stays off until step 4. Hermes sends no message to your customers before you say you are ready.

Before you start

  • Access to your WATI account, including the API Docs page.
  • The name of your WATI plan.
  • The person who controls your WhatsApp Business Account in Meta, available for step 3.
  • Your 41 Labs contact, for each step.
  • About 10 minutes for step 3.

Step by step

01

Give Hermes access to WATI

  1. Confirm to your 41 Labs contact that Hermes may pull your WhatsApp chat history from WATI.
  2. Tell your 41 Labs contact your WATI plan.
  3. In WATI, open the API Docs page and send the API details to your 41 Labs contact.
Who replies to customersYour staff, in WATI
Your AI assistantOff
02

Let Hermes learn from your chats

Hermes reads your chats, old and new, and learns your products, prices and style. There is nothing for you to do in this step.

  1. Keep replying to customers in WATI as usual.
Who replies to customersYour staff, in WATI
Your AI assistantOff. It only learns.
03

Move the number to Hermes

  1. Find the person who controls your WhatsApp Business Account in Meta.
  2. That person signs in to Hermes and clicks Connect WhatsApp. This takes about 10 minutes.
  3. From now on, reply to customers in the Hermes Inbox. All past chats are already there.
  4. Stop using WATI.

Your customers keep the same number, and they see no break in the chat. Customers who asked you to stop messaging them stay opted out.

Who replies to customersYour staff, in Hermes
Your AI assistantOff
04

Go live

  1. Try the AI on real customer questions with your 41 Labs contact.
  2. Tell your 41 Labs contact when you are ready to go live. 41 Labs turns the AI on.
  3. When the AI needs help, it sends the chat to your staff in the Inbox.
Who replies to customersThe AI, with your staff as backup
Your AI assistantOn
