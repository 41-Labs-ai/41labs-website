Connecting WESS

Connecting your WESS bookings

At the end of this guide, your AI takes booking requests for your outlets and hands them to your team, or sends your WESS booking link.

Your AI does not book into WESS directly yet. Your team books each request in WESS. Step 6 asks WESS to open access.

Before you start

You need:

  • A WESS login that can export your customer list.
  • Your WESS booking link, if you want your AI to send it.
  • Your 41 Sales Team dashboard, with the owner or admin role.
  • About 10 minutes.

Step by step

01

Open WESS on the Connections screen

  1. In your dashboard, go to Settings → Connections.
  2. Under Add more, find WESS. The Booking chip shows booking systems only.
  3. Press Connect.
02

Choose how your AI books for now

Under How your AI books for now, pick one:

Hand the booking to your teamYour AI takes the treatment, outlet and time the customer wants, tells them your team will confirm, and alerts your team on WhatsApp.
Send your WESS booking linkYour AI answers questions, then sends your link so the customer picks a time in WESS.

If you pick the link, paste it into Your WESS booking link. It is the link in your Instagram bio or Google profile, starting with https://.

  1. Under Your outlets, type your outlet names, separated by commas.
  2. Press Connect WESS.
03

Export your customers from WESS

  1. In WESS, export your customer list as a CSV file.
  2. Save the file on your computer.

If you cannot find the export in WESS, ask your WESS account manager where it is.

04

Upload your customer list

  1. On the WESS page, under Your customer list, press Upload WESS export and pick the file.
  2. Check the counts: new, updated and skipped. A skipped row shows its reason.
  3. Press Import. The button shows how many customers it will import.

Your AI then greets returning customers by name. Nobody is sent a message.

05

Ask WESS for access

  1. On the WESS page, under Where this is, press Ask WESS for access.
  2. Send your WESS account manager the message the screen shows:
Please give 41 Labs API access to our WESS account, so our AI assistant can see free times and book appointments for us.
06

Test it from your own phone

  1. From a phone that is not an admin number, message your business WhatsApp number and ask to book a treatment.
  2. Check your WhatsApp: your team got the booking request, or the customer got your WESS link.

Checklist

Good to know

  • Upload a fresh export every month, so new customers are greeted as returning customers.
  • Your AI never confirms a time itself while it hands bookings to your team.
  • Press Disconnect to stop. Nothing changes in WESS.
