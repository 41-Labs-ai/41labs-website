Connecting Plato

Connecting your Plato account

At the end of this guide, your AI shows real free times from Plato and books patients in the way you choose.

Only a Plato admin can make an API key. If you are not the Plato admin, do Step 1 together with them.

Before you start

You need:

  • A Plato login with the admin role.
  • Your 41 Sales Team dashboard, with the owner or admin role.
  • About 10 minutes.
  • A phone that is not set up as an admin number on your dashboard, for the test in Step 9.

What we ask you for

FieldRequired?Where to find it
Clinic account in PlatoYesPlato's name for your clinic's database. Plato support can confirm it.
API keyYesIn Plato: System Setup → General Settings → API, then Generate API Key. Treat it like a password.

Step by step

01

Make an API key in Plato

  1. In Plato, open System Setup → General Settings.
  2. Open the API tab.
  3. Press Generate API Key.
  4. Copy the key.

Send the key to nobody. Paste it into your dashboard only.

02

Open Plato on the Connections screen

  1. In your dashboard, go to Settings → Connections.
  2. Under Add more, find Plato. The Booking chip shows booking systems only.
  3. Press Connect.
03

Enter your clinic account and key

  1. Type your clinic account in Clinic account in Plato.
  2. Paste the key into API key.
  3. Press Test the connection.
04

Check the free times are yours

The screen lists the calendars it found in Plato and up to three free times.

  1. Open your Plato diary for the same day.
  2. Check that each time shown is free in Plato.

If you see a message instead:

Plato did not accept this keyCopy the key again in full, or make a new one in Step 1.
No API accessAsk Plato support to turn on API access for your clinic.
Plato did not answerWait a few minutes, then press Test the connection again.
05

Name the calendars your patients know

  1. Next to each Plato calendar, type the name your patients use, such as the therapist or the room.
  2. Switch off any calendar your AI must never book, such as admin blocks.
  3. Press Next: choose who books.
06

Choose who books

Under Who books, pick one:

Your AI books, your team confirmsThe time is held in Plato as not yet confirmed. The patient hears your team will confirm. Your team gets a WhatsApp alert.
Your AI books itThe appointment goes into Plato as confirmed. The patient hears it is booked.
Your team books itYour AI shows real free times and takes the patient's choice. Your team books it in Plato.
07

Choose when a person steps in

Under Hand to a person when, switch on the rules you want:

  • The patient is new: their WhatsApp number is not in Plato yet. Your team creates the record and books.
  • A change or cancel comes in late: pick 12, 24 or 48 hours before the appointment.

Press Connect Plato. The Plato page opens, and the card moves to Connected.

08

Check your opening hours

  1. On the Plato page, under Opening hours, check the times for each day.
  2. Switch off the days you are closed. They show Closed.
  3. Under Each appointment, pick the length of one appointment.
  4. Press Save hours.

Your AI offers times inside these hours only.

09

Test it from your own phone

  1. From your test phone, message your business WhatsApp number and ask for an appointment.
  2. Pick one of the times your AI offers.
  3. Check Plato: the appointment shows on the calendar you named.
  4. Check your WhatsApp: your team got the booking alert, if you chose a team step.

Checklist

Good to know

  • Your AI never asks a patient for an NRIC. A new patient is added with name and phone only.
  • A patient can see, move or cancel only appointments that match their own WhatsApp number.
  • When Plato does not answer, your AI hands the booking to your team and never guesses a time.
  • Press Disconnect to stop. Appointments already made stay in Plato.
  • To change the key, make a new one in Plato and enter it again on the Plato screen.
