Connecting your booking backend

Your backend pushes every price, vehicle and charge to the AI sales agent, so the agent quotes live figures instead of reading a spreadsheet.

For your in-house developer · v3 · 11 September 2026

The method is a real-time webhook push. Your backend calls one 41 Labs endpoint each time a price, a vehicle or a picture changes. The change reaches the agent at once.

Who does what

You send An HTTP POST to the endpoint below on every create, update or delete of a vehicle, a price or a charge. You retry on failure and you store the secret.
41 Labs gives The endpoint URL, a secret token, and a test endpoint to dry-run against. We validate each payload and sync it into the agent at once.

The endpoint

You receive one HTTPS URL and one bearer token. Send:

POST https://aisalescloser.41labs.ai/api/webhooks/hertz/sync
Authorization: Bearer <secret-token>
Content-Type: application/json

A 200 OK reply means we accepted the change. On any other reply, retry with backoff — for example after 1, 5 and 30 minutes.

A change that never arrives means the agent quotes a wrong price to a customer. Retry until the endpoint answers 200 OK.

Send over HTTPS only. Store the token as an environment variable, not in code.

The events

Every request carries one of these three event types:

EventSend it when
vehicle.upserted A vehicle was created or changed — price, picture, model or insurance.
vehicle.deleted A vehicle is no longer offered.
charges.updated The shared charges changed — delivery, surcharges, deposits or blackout periods.

The fields: the rate breaks

One rate break per duration band. The daily price drops the longer the rental runs.

FieldWhat it carries
min_daysBand start, inclusive. Example 1.
max_daysBand end. null means open ended.
base_dailyThe pre-GST daily rate in SGD. Example 128.00.
gst_pctGST percent, stated separately. Example 9.
currencyExample "SGD".

On the sheet the bands run 1 / 3 / 7 / 14 / 30 days and Base is pre-GST. Send base_daily and gst_pct; we compute the totals.

Dates and the price

This one is still open, and you choose the format. A car can cost more in a peak season, and less off-peak. The fields above price a rental by its length, and say nothing about the date.

Tell us which of these you have:

  • A daily rate that changes by date range: peak, off-peak, school holidays, year end.
  • A surcharge that lands on a date, and whether it applies per day or per rental.
  • A public holiday rate, and the calendar that names the holidays.
  • A blackout period that carries real dates, not only a name.
  • A minimum hire length on some dates.
  • A rate with a start date and an end date, sent before it starts.

This applies to every brand you run: Hertz and Ace Drive. If the two brands price a season differently, send the dates for each brand.

Send us what you already hold, and we adjust to it. A date range on a rate break, a season table, a spreadsheet tab, or an export. Any shape works.

Send one example of each price that moves with the calendar. Your own format is enough. We write the mapping and send it back to you.

If your backend does not hold the dates, tell us where they live today. We read them from that source.

Until this is settled the agent quotes the same daily rate on every date.

Availability

This one is still open, and it decides what the agent can answer. The backend holds price and picture, not inventory. Availability sits in the RBF spreadsheet, and in a separate tab for Trip.com.

Tell us which source the agent should read:

  • Availability also enters your backend, and arrives on the same webhook. This is the one we recommend.
  • Availability stays in the RBF sheet, and we read the backend and the sheet together.
  • Another source, which you name.

Until this is settled the agent can quote a price, but it cannot say a car is available.
