Connecting your payment webhook

Your booking system relays every settlement to Hermes, so a booking marks itself paid, failed or expired the moment the payment resolves.

For your in-house developer · v1 · 19 September 2026

You relay, you do not forward. Your booking system receives the settlement webhook at its own /v1/payments/webhook, then sends a fresh request to Hermes in the shape below. Hermes validates your payload and your token, not a raw payment-provider signature.

Who does what

You send An HTTP POST to the endpoint below each time a payment settles, fails or expires. You store the token and you retry on failure.
41 Labs gives The endpoint URL and a bearer token. We validate each payload, match it to the booking and update it at once.

The endpoint

Send over HTTPS only:

POST https://aisalescloser.41labs.ai/api/webhooks/druk-trs/cmscl2z2v0001253rseqhzcst
Authorization: Bearer <token>
Content-Type: application/json

The token is static and 41 Labs issues it. Store it as an environment variable, not in code.

When you send

Relay on all three outcomes, not only on success:

StatusSend it when
paidThe payment settled.
failedThe payment was declined or errored.
expiredThe payment link or intent ran out of time.

A failure or an expiry that never arrives leaves the booking sitting as unpaid with no reason attached. Send all three.

The fields

FieldTypeRequiredWhat it carries
eventstringYes One of payment.paid, payment.failed, payment.expired.
online_booking_idstringYes Echo it back from the payment. Hermes matches the booking on this.
booking_ref_codestringYes Echo it back from the payment. Your reconciliation key.
payment_referencestringYes The payment intent or transaction id. This is the idempotency key.
statusenumYes paid, failed or expired.
amountnumberYes The settled amount.
currencystringYes ISO 4217, for example "USD".
paid_atstringOn paid Settlement time, ISO 8601 in UTC.

The reply, and retries

A success looks like this:

200 { "received": true }

Any reply that is not a 2xx is a failure. Retry with backoff — for example after 1, 5 and 30 minutes.

Hermes dedupes on payment_reference, so sending the same event twice is safe. Retry until you receive a 2xx.

What we need from you next

01

Store the token

Take the bearer token from your 41 Labs contact and store it as an environment variable. Send it on every request as Authorization: Bearer <token>.

02

Build the relay

At your own /v1/payments/webhook, build the payload above and POST it to the 41 Labs endpoint. Echo online_booking_id and booking_ref_code straight back from the payment.

03

Cover all three outcomes

Relay on paid, on failed and on expired. Set event and status to match, and send paid_at on a settlement.

04

Add retries with backoff

Treat any non-2xx reply as a failure and retry. Keep payment_reference the same across retries of one event, so Hermes recognises the resend.

05

Send one live event and confirm the booking moved

Run one real payment through, watch for the 200, then tell your 41 Labs contact the booking_ref_code. We confirm the booking marked itself paid on our side.
