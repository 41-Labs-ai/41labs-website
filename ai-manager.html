Questions

What owners ask about the AI Manager.

What is the AI Manager?

It is the part of 41 Sales Team that watches your WhatsApp sales for you. It shows revenue closed, deals and who needs a reply on a sales dashboard, and it alerts the right person on your team on WhatsApp when a customer needs one.

What does the sales dashboard show?

Revenue closed, deals closed, your return, and how many leads became sales. Under that, the number of active leads, new leads today, chats that need a reply, and a pipeline board with every customer by stage.

How does it tell my staff when a customer needs them?

It sends a WhatsApp alert to the person who owns the chat, with the customer's question and a link to the chat. They swipe-reply the alert, and their answer goes to the customer. If the chat has no owner, every admin gets the alert.

What happens if nobody answers?

If the owner has not replied within the wait you set, every admin gets a no reply yet message. If a question still waits on your team after an hour, your admins get a reminder on WhatsApp.

Can I ask it questions on WhatsApp?

Yes. Send a plain message to the alert number, such as who is waiting on a reply, or how many leads came in today. It answers from your live data. In the dashboard, the Co-pilot answers the same kind of question in more detail.

Can my staff see only their own chats?

Yes. Staff open the inbox on Mine, which shows only the chats they own. Owners and admins see everything, and the dashboard shows how many chats each person owns, how many became sales and how fast they reply.

How much does it cost?

It is part of every 41 Sales Team plan, so there is no separate fee. Plans start small and grow with the number of conversations. See pricing, or we work out the right plan with you on the call.
