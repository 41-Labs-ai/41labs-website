What it does

What it watches and tells you.

Every item below is in your dashboard or on your WhatsApp today.

A sales dashboard

Revenue closed, deals closed, your return on the plan and how many leads became sales. Pick this month, a date range or all time.

Counts the sales

Card payments taken in the chat count by themselves. When a customer says they paid by bank transfer or cash, it spots it and asks you to confirm.

Your pipeline at a glance

Every customer sits in a column by stage, so you see who is new, who is deciding and who has paid.

Alerts the right person on WhatsApp

When a customer needs a person, the chat's owner gets a WhatsApp alert first. They swipe-reply the alert and the answer goes to the customer.

Tells the admins if nobody answers

If the owner has not replied within the wait you set, 15 minutes unless you change it, every admin gets a no reply yet message with a link to the chat.

Answers your questions on WhatsApp

Send "who is waiting on a reply?" or "how many leads today?" to the alert number and it answers from your live data.

A Co-pilot in your dashboard

Ask in plain words how many leads came in this week, where customers drop off, or what one customer wanted. It reads your own chats and numbers.

Shows how the AI is doing

The AI Health page shows how often the AI hands a chat to your team, how fast your team answers, and what is still waiting.

Questions

What owners ask about the AI Manager.

What is the AI Manager?

It is the part of 41 Sales Team that watches your WhatsApp sales for you. It shows revenue closed, deals and who needs a reply on a sales dashboard, and it alerts the right person on your team on WhatsApp when a customer needs one.

What does the sales dashboard show?

Revenue closed, deals closed, your return on the plan, and how many leads became sales. Under that, the number of active leads, new leads today, chats that need a reply, and a pipeline board with every customer by stage.

How does it tell my staff when a customer needs them?

It sends a WhatsApp alert to the person who owns the chat, with the customer's question and a link to the chat. They swipe-reply the alert, and their answer goes to the customer. If the chat has no owner, every admin gets the alert.

What happens if nobody answers?

If the owner has not replied within the wait you set, every admin gets a no reply yet message. If a question still waits on your team after an hour, your admins get a reminder on WhatsApp.

Can I ask it questions on WhatsApp?

Yes. Send a plain message to the alert number, such as who is waiting on a reply, or how many leads came in today. It answers from your live data. In the dashboard, the Co-pilot answers the same kind of question in more detail.

Can my staff see only their own chats?

Yes. Staff open the inbox on Mine, which shows only the chats they own. Owners and admins see everything, and the dashboard shows how many chats each person owns, how many became sales and how fast they reply.

How much does it cost?

It is included in every 41 Sales Team plan, from S$299 a month. There is no separate fee for the AI Manager.
