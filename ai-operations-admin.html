What it does

The after-sales work it does for you.

Every item below runs on your WhatsApp number today.

Sends the invoice after payment

When the customer pays through the link in the chat, the invoice goes back to them on WhatsApp. It also sends one whenever a customer asks for it.

Saves what customers tell it

Dates, addresses, names and needs go on the customer's record as the chat goes. It also reads the photos and PDFs they send.

Moves and cancels bookings

When your booking system is connected, it finds the customer's own booking, moves it to a free time or cancels it. Late changes can go to your team instead.

Checks in on the date they ask for

A customer says "message me after the holidays". It sets a check-in for that date and sends it then. You see the date in the chat.

Chases quiet customers

It follows up when a customer goes quiet. After 24 hours it uses WhatsApp-approved messages, which we set up for you. No follow-up goes out in your quiet hours.

Messages your past customers

Pick a tag, such as renewal due, and it messages everyone with it. Or set a date field, and each customer gets a reminder when their date comes near.

Gives each chat an owner

New chats go to the right person on your team, by country or by taking turns. That person is the first one alerted.

Hands a chat to your staff

Your staff can take over a chat at any time, from the dashboard or from WhatsApp. Type /ai and it carries on.

Questions

What owners ask about the AI Operations Admin.

What does the AI Operations Admin do?

It handles the work after a customer pays on WhatsApp. It sends the invoice, saves the details the customer gives, moves or cancels bookings when your booking system is connected, checks in on the date the customer asks for, and hands the chat to your staff when they are needed.

Does it send invoices?

Yes. When a customer pays through the payment link in the chat, the invoice goes back to them on WhatsApp. It also sends one when a customer asks for an invoice for their records.

Can it change or cancel a booking?

Yes, when your booking system is connected. It finds the customer's own booking by their WhatsApp number, moves it to a free time or cancels it, and tells them exactly what happened. You can send late changes to your team instead.

Is it an AI receptionist?

On WhatsApp, it does a lot of the front desk work after a sale. It sends invoices, saves the details customers give, checks in when they asked, and moves bookings when your booking system is connected. It works on WhatsApp only and does not answer phone calls.

How do follow-ups work after 24 hours?

WhatsApp only allows approved messages once a customer has been quiet for 24 hours. We set those messages up on your account for you, so every follow-up still goes out. Nothing goes out in your quiet hours, and it stops for anyone who says stop.

Can it message my past customers?

Yes. Pick a tag, such as renewal due, and it messages every customer with that tag. Or pick a date field, such as an expiry date, and each customer gets a reminder when their date comes near. People who opted out are always skipped.

How much does it cost?

It is included in every 41 Sales Team plan, from S$299 a month. There is no separate fee for the AI Operations Admin.
