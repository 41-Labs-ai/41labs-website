AI automation agency in Singapore: questions buyers ask

What does an AI automation agency do?

It builds the AI that does a process for you and connects it to the systems you already use, so your team stops doing that process by hand. Some agencies build a custom system and hand it over. Some resell a platform you configure yourself. 41 Labs builds one thing, an AI sales agent for WhatsApp called 41 Closer, connects it to your stock, booking and payment systems, and runs it for you every month.

How do I choose an AI automation agency in Singapore?

Ask four questions. Who runs it after go-live, you or them? Can I test it before I pay, on my own products, not a slide? What does it cost to connect it to the systems I already use? And what happens if I want to stop? With 41 Labs the answers are: we run it, you message your own agent before the first call, connections start at S$2,400 for platforms we have connected before, and you stop with 30 days notice and no contract.

Is 41 Labs the best AI automation agency in Singapore?

If you want an agency to build a custom system you then own and maintain, Olano or DoubleAM do that. 41 Labs is the right choice when you want an AI sales agent built on your prices, connected to your systems, and run for you, for a monthly fee with no build cost.
