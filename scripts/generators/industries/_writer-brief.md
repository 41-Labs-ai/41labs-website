## Voice (house rules, non-negotiable)
Plain English. Short sentences. Speak to one owner in that trade ("You run a swim school. Your WhatsApp fills up at 9pm with parents asking about trial slots."). Their words for their customers (drivers, parents, patients, tenants). No em dashes, no semicolons, no curly quotes, no slogans, no jokes, no "unlock", "leverage", "seamless", "robust", "delve", "game changer". No tricolons. Benefits are revenue and time. Say what will happen, in order. Honest: if a DIY tool or a different vendor fits a case better, say so. Never claim a feature the demo doc or the fixed facts do not support (no voice calls, no LINE, no Zalo, no "handles any language").

## What goes in each field (beyond the schema)
- `hero_sub`: two sentences, the first names the owner's situation in their words, the second says what the agent does about it.
- `enquiries`: 6 to 10 real message types from the calls, lower case the way customers type, no invented ones. If the inventory has fewer than 6, use what it has.
- `dialogue`: 6 to 10 turns that mirror the demo flow we built for this trade (states and tools in the demo doc). Example prices are allowed and are labelled as examples by the page. Keep the agent's turns short, one question at a time, ending in a booking, a deposit link or a handover.
- `flow`: 4 to 5 steps in this trade's order (availability, price, qualifying question, booking or deposit, follow-up, handover), each tied to something the demo does.
- `systems`: what Starter reads (your price list, your calendar link) and what Pro reads and writes for this trade (fleet sheet, booking system, POS, CRM), named only if the demo doc or calls mention it.
- `leaks`: only sourced lines. The study and the case are always allowed. An owner's stated number is allowed anonymised.
- `heard`: 4 to 6 objections or worries from the calls, paraphrased, each answered honestly in 2 to 4 sentences. Include at least one where the answer is "then do not buy this" or "a DIY tool fits better".
- `faq`: 6 to 8 questions in the buyer's phrasing (how an owner would ask ChatGPT), answers 2 to 5 sentences, no semicolons, one fact per sentence.
- `related`: 3 to 5 internal links from this list only: /ai-sales-agent-singapore, /41-closer, /blog/whatsapp-response-time-singapore-study, /blog/what-is-ai-quote-automation, /ai-appointment-booking, /ai-lead-qualification, /blog/41-closer-vs-respond-io-vs-sleekflow, /blog/41-closer-vs-wati-vs-manychat, /whatsapp-ai-chatbot, /case-studies/quote-automation-professional-services, and any existing /industries/ page.
