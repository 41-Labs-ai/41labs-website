Plan Terms & Fair Use

Last updated: 1 October 2026

← Back to pricing

This page explains, in plain English, how 41 Closer plans, AI usage, and billing work, including what the "new customers a month" on each plan is based on. It sits alongside the order or statement of work you sign. If anything here conflicts with a signed agreement, the signed agreement takes precedence.

What you're paying for

41 Closer is a managed service. The build is included in every plan, so there is no setup fee. You pay a monthly plan while we build, run, and tune it for you. You are not buying software that you operate. We operate it for you.

How usage works (the credit pool)

Everything the AI reads and writes runs on credits. Every plan includes a set number of credits each month.

One new customer, from first message to the sale, uses about 200,000 credits on average. That covers the AI reading its instructions and the relevant parts of your catalogue and prices, plus the whole back-and-forth with your customer and any follow-ups. Short enquiries use fewer; long or complex ones use more.

So the "new customers a month" shown on each plan is an estimate, worked out as your monthly credit allowance divided by about 200,000 credits per customer:

Pay for a year up front and you get twelve months for the price of ten: $6,900 Starter, $14,900 Pro, $43,000 Scale, $79,000 Custom. Same plan, same credits each month. Still 30 days notice, and we refund the unused whole months if you stop.

If you go over your allowance

It keeps answering. We tell you when you reach 70% of your credits and again when you reach the limit, so you can move up a plan. Anything over is charged at $4 per 100,000 credits, which is about $8 per extra customer that month, and we move you up a plan the next month. We will never charge overage without telling you first. If usage reaches three times your allowance in a month the AI pauses and we call you, so a runaway month cannot quietly become a large bill.

Connecting your own systems

The build is included. The only one-time cost is connecting 41 Closer to a system you already run, so it can read live stock and prices and write orders and bookings back. Scoping that is always free.

  • No charge: Google Calendar, Outlook, Stripe, Airwallex, Attio, Twenty, Google Sheets, WhatsApp, Messenger, SMS, email, webhooks, and loading your catalogue.
  • $4,800 for the first, $2,400 for one we have connected before: a platform with a normal API, for example Shopify or a common booking or accounting system.
  • From $9,600: a system with no usable API, or one that needs custom work. Charged at $1,200 a day with an eight day minimum, quoted before we start.

Each connected system beyond the first adds $150 a month to keep it working as that system changes. An extra brand, number, or price list is $2,400 to set up and carries its own plan.

Billing and payment

  • Plans are billed monthly in advance, in Singapore Dollars (SGD), by card or bank transfer.
  • Billing starts the day it goes live, not the day you sign.
  • One month is the only commitment. No GST is charged.

Cancellation

After the first month, plans are month to month with 30 days notice. There is no contract and no cancellation fee. When you cancel, we keep the agent running until the end of the period you have paid for, then switch it off.

Guarantees

You can watch 41 Closer sell your own products before you commit, and we check your numbers with you first at no charge. Beyond that there is no guarantee. No refund, no free month, no service credit. We cannot control your revenue, so we would rather you judge it on what it does in your own WhatsApp than on a promise.

What we run, and availability

We build, run, and tune the agent for you and keep it on brand and on price. When a chat is tricky, or a customer wants a person, the agent hands off to your team with the full history. We aim for high availability and monitor the service. A formal uptime SLA is available on Custom plans. WhatsApp itself is provided by Meta and is subject to Meta's own availability and policies.

Your data, and who owns what

You own your agent and everything it learns. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it except as needed to run the service (for example, the AI providers and the WhatsApp and payment platforms that power it). We handle personal data in line with Singapore's PDPA. You are responsible for the accuracy of the products, prices, and information you give us to train the agent.

Fair use

Each plan is for one business's genuine customer conversations. Plans may not be used for spam, unsolicited bulk messaging, reselling the service, or anything that breaks WhatsApp's or Meta's Business rules. We may pause a service that breaches these rules or Meta's policies.

EDG grant

Eligible Singapore companies may be able to offset part of the cost through the Enterprise Development Grant (EDG), and we can help with the paperwork. Grant approval is decided by Enterprise Singapore, not by us, and is not guaranteed.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms as the product changes. If a change is material and affects your plan, we will tell you before it takes effect.
